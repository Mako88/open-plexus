Is the distractor failure structural, or was it measured too early?
====================================================================

Script: experiments/g39_03_is_the_distractor_failure_structural.py
Status: PREDICTIONS WRITTEN, run pending. Committed before execution.


IN PLAIN TERMS
--------------
There is one stubborn failure in this whole line of work. Something that is
present in every single moment -- a hum in the room, the word "the" -- gets
treated as though it were meaningful, because it turns up alongside everything.

Five different things have been tried against it and none worked. So the working
conclusion has been that it is a deep problem rather than a tuning problem.

**But there is a reason to doubt that conclusion, and it appeared only
yesterday.** All five of those attempts were measured after three thousand
moments -- and we have since found the machine was still learning fast at that
point. One of the two mechanisms being compared turned out to be much further
from its final state than the other. So an idea that looked refuted might simply
have been measured before its benefit arrived.

That is the most expensive mistake this project has a name for: throwing away a
good idea because the measurement was wrong.

So this re-runs the two most important of those attempts at the length where the
machine has actually stopped improving, and crosses them together, which nothing
has done. If nothing works there either, "it is a deep problem" stops being a
summary of five disappointments and becomes a claim with real evidence behind it.
If something does work, five conclusions need revisiting.

Either answer is worth having. The one that would be embarrassing is not asking.


WHAT IS BEING MEASURED
----------------------
    link         precision over the top-`k` image codes a word reaches
    covered      how many of the codes it SHOULD find it had a chance to score
    distractor   share of words admitting the ever-present distractor
    BOTH?        the whole question, as one column: link >= 0.80 AND
                 covered >= 0.80 AND distractor <= 0.05

**The coverage clause in `BOTH?` is not decoration.** `g38-03` reported a cell at
1.0000 precision over 0.0867 coverage as though it were the best in the table --
a mechanism that is right about a twentieth of the answer is not solving
anything. A criterion without that clause is the one `g36-06` wrote and then had
to retract, where a cell met the bar at a collapsed graph.


MEASURED ON
-----------
Local, one process. Scorer and stream construction match `g39-01` and `g39-02`.

    images       4,000   MNIST prefix
    recordings   3,000   FSDD, six speakers
    occasions   24,000   extended by RE-PAIRING, since an exact repeat is a
                         no-op for a ratio statistic
    codes           50   pinned, as everywhere in this line
    arm      together    where the failure is sharpest
    beam/depth   16, 1   g38-03's operating point
    seeds      0, 1, 2

Swept: `alpha` in {0.5, 0.75, 1.0} x `combiner` in {min, geometric, mean, max},
at 3,000 and 24,000 occasions. **The two axes have never been crossed.**

**Alpha 0 and 0.25 are excluded on evidence, not cost:** `g36-06` measured them
admitting the distractor at 1.0000 and 0.9333, which is the failing end of the
axis being probed.


PREDICTIONS
-----------
P1  **THE HEADLINE, WRITTEN TO FAIL.** No setting meets `BOTH?` at either
    length. `solved` is 0 of 24.
    REFUTED IF any does. **That would be the best outcome available in this
    entire line** -- it would revise `g36-06` and `g38-01`, and it would mean
    five axes of mechanism work were chasing something a longer stream and a
    crossed grid fix by themselves.

P2  THE 3,000 ROWS REPRODUCE WHAT WAS ALREADY REPORTED. `mean` at alpha 1.0 and
    3,000 occasions gives link near 0.9665 and distractor 1.0000, matching
    `g38-01` and `g38-03`.
    REFUTED IF it does not. **That is the rail** -- a mismatch means the stream
    construction has drifted from the runs being re-checked and nothing else
    here is comparable.

P3  MORE DAMPING SUPPRESSES THE DISTRACTOR AND COSTS THE LINK, at both lengths,
    for every combiner. The two columns move in opposite directions along alpha.
    REFUTED IF they move together anywhere -- which is exactly the interior the
    first two sweeps failed to find, and would locate it.

P4  CONVERGENCE HELPS THE STRICT COMBINERS MOST. `min` and `geometric` improve
    more between 3,000 and 24,000 than `mean` does, because `g39-02` found the
    arm furthest from its plateau gains most, and those two scored worst at
    3,000.
    REFUTED IF `mean` gains most, which would mean the permissive end was the
    under-measured one and the ordering at 3,000 was already fair.

P5  DISTRACTOR ADMISSION IS UNCHANGED BY LENGTH for every setting -- whatever it
    is at 3,000 it is at 24,000, within 0.05.
    REFUTED IF it moves. That would mean the failure is partly an evidence
    effect after all, and `g39-02`'s conclusion that experience does not repair
    it was drawn from one setting rather than from the mechanism.
