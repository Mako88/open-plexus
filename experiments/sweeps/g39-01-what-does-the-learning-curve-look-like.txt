Is the grounding result a plateau, or a snapshot of a curve still moving?
=========================================================================

Script: experiments/g39_01_what_does_the_learning_curve_look_like.py
Status: RUN, local, 2026-07-31, 8.6s. Predictions committed at `f1145ae`
        before the script was executed.

ANSWER: **NOT A PLATEAU. IT IS STILL CLIMBING STEEPLY WHEN THE DATA RUNS OUT.**
        `link` goes **0.5643 at 1,500 occasions to 0.9665 at 3,000** — a rise of
        **0.40 over the last doubling**, against a refutation threshold of 0.05.
        The curve is steepest at the very end of the stream.

        **So every absolute grounding figure in `g32` through `g38` is a LOWER
        BOUND that was reported as a value.** Comparisons between arms at equal
        stream length survive; absolute claims and "X does not help" claims do
        not. See WHAT THIS INVALIDATES below, enumerated before anything is
        built on top of it.

        Four of five predictions refuted. The one that held is the rail.


IN PLAIN TERMS
--------------
Everything this project has measured about grounding was scored **once, at the
end**. Pour in three thousand moments, then ask how well it did.

That is the wrong shape for what this is supposed to be, and the project's own
goals document says so plainly: there is no training phase and no operating
phase. There is one stream of experience and the thing learns from it forever.
Splitting it into "learn" and then "test" measures a system that stops, which is
exactly what this design forbids.

So this changes the measurement rather than the mechanism. **The mechanism was
already continuous** — it updates as each moment arrives and nothing about it
ever stops. What was batch-shaped was us, asking only at the end.

Now it gets asked all the way along. Every result becomes a curve instead of a
dot.

**And that answers a question nobody has asked out loud.** We report that it
identifies the right pictures 97% of the time. Is that where it *settles*, or
just where it happened to be when we ran out of data? Those mean very different
things. If the curve is still climbing at the end, then every number we have is a
floor being quoted as a result — and this project has made close to that mistake
before, running an entire sweep in a range where nothing was moving any more.

The other thing it shows is how *fast* it learns. If a concept is solid after
twenty examples, that is a completely different kind of system from one that
needs three thousand. Nobody here knows which it is.


WHAT IS BEING MEASURED
----------------------
    link         of the top-`k` image codes a word reaches, the share whose
                 majority digit is that word's, computed from the table AS IT
                 STANDS at that point in the stream
    covered      how many of the codes it SHOULD find it had a chance to score.
                 **`link` is not readable without it** -- `g38-03` reported
                 1.0000 over 8% coverage as though it were the best cell
    partition    the incumbent `equivalence_classes`, scored at the same points,
                 so the two mechanisms have the same curve and not just the
                 same endpoint
    distractor   share of words admitting the ever-present distractor
    surfaces     how many surfaces exist at all yet. **The rail**: early rows
                 score a world that has not finished arriving

**There is no held-out set and nothing is replayed.** The ground truth is the
quantiser's own majority label, which is not part of the mechanism at any point.
That is what makes this prequential rather than a train/test curve renamed.

**Checkpoints are geometric, not even** — 25, 50, 100, 200, 400, 800, 1500,
3000. A learning curve should move most at the start, and an even grid spends its
resolution where nothing is happening. That is `g11-05`'s failure seen from the
other end: five points, fifteen jobs, every one past saturation.


MEASURED ON
-----------
Local, one process. Streams built INLINE rather than by `g36_04._stream`, because
that function observes the whole stream before returning — which is exactly the
batch shape this run exists to stop doing. The occasion CONTENTS are identical.

    images       4,000   MNIST prefix
    recordings   3,000   FSDD, six speakers
    occasions    3,000   one pass, scored at 8 points
    codes           50   pinned from g36-04
    noise            2   imported from g36_04, so it cannot drift
    distractors      1   present EVERY occasion
    beam            16   `g38-03`'s operating point
    depth            1   `g38-03` found deeper is worse and dearer
    combiner      mean   `g38-01`'s winner
    seeds      0, 1, 2

Arms: `together` and `alternating`.

**The quantiser is fitted on all 4,000 images before the stream starts**, and
that is a real inconsistency with the prequential claim. It is left in and named
rather than fixed, because fixing it means an online quantiser, which does not
exist. **So this is prequential in the LINKING and batch in the PERCEPTION**, and
no row should be read as though the whole system were online.


PREDICTIONS
-----------
P1  IT LEARNS FAST AND THEN STOPS. `link` reaches 90% of its final value by 800
    occasions — 80 per digit — and moves by less than 0.05 between 1500 and 3000.
    REFUTED IF it is still climbing by more than 0.05 over the last doubling.
    **That is the outcome that matters most**: it would mean every grounding
    figure in this project is a lower bound reported as a value, and the stream
    length becomes a variable that has been silently held constant across g32
    through g38.

P2  COVERAGE ARRIVES LATER THAN PRECISION. `covered` reaches its plateau after
    `link` does, because being right about the few codes seen early is easier
    than having seen all of them.
    REFUTED IF coverage saturates first, which would mean the mechanism finds
    everything immediately and spends the rest of the stream sorting it.

P3  THE WALK BEATS THE PARTITION AT EVERY POINT, not just at the end. `link`
    exceeds `partition` at all eight checkpoints in `together`.
    REFUTED IF the partition leads early and loses later. That would mean the
    hard cut is the better rule when evidence is thin and the advantage measured
    in `g38-01` is a large-data effect — which would change when to use which,
    rather than which to use.

P4  THE DISTRACTOR IS ADMITTED FROM THE VERY FIRST CHECKPOINT. `distractor` is
    1.0000 at 25 occasions and never falls.
    REFUTED IF it starts low and rises, or starts high and falls. Either would
    mean the failure is a function of how much has been seen, and therefore
    something experience could fix — which four axes have so far failed to.

P5  **THE RAIL.** `surfaces` is well below its final value at 25 occasions, so
    the earliest rows describe a world still arriving and must not be read as
    performance.
    REFUTED IF `surfaces` is already complete at 25, which would mean the early
    checkpoints are measuring a fully-populated world and the curve is purely
    about evidence rather than about coverage of the world.


COST
----
Estimated from `g38-03`'s measured 7.2s for 15 scoring cells: this scores 8
checkpoints x 2 arms x 3 seeds = 48 cells, plus `equivalence_classes` at each,
which is the dearer of the two. **30 to 120 seconds**, local.

Recorded after the run: **8.6s**, below a 30-120s estimate. The streams are
built once per seed and scored in place, so the checkpoints cost almost nothing
beyond the single pass they interrupt.

SCORECARD
---------
  P1  **REFUTED, BY EIGHT TIMES THE THRESHOLD, AND IT IS THE FINDING.** The
            prediction was 90% of final by 800 occasions and under 0.05 of
            movement over the last doubling.

            Actual: **0.3710 at 800**, which is 38% of final, and the last
            doubling moves **0.5643 to 0.9665** — a rise of **0.4022**.

            **The curve is not merely unfinished, it is steepest at the end.**
            Every earlier row is near chance until 400 occasions, then it climbs
            without slowing.

            This file's own prediction says what that means: *"every grounding
            figure in this project is a lower bound reported as a value, and the
            stream length becomes a variable that has been silently held
            constant across g32 through g38."* That is now the case, and stream
            length is exactly `CLAUDE.md`'s *"a variable that never changes does
            not look like a variable — it looks like the background."*

  P2  **REFUTED, BACKWARDS, and the inversion is informative.** Coverage was
            predicted to arrive after precision. It arrives **first and almost
            immediately**: 0.6733 at 25 occasions and 0.8867 at 100, while
            `link` is still at 0.1050.

            **So the mechanism finds nearly everything at once and spends the
            entire stream sorting it.** That is a different system from the one
            assumed — the work is discrimination, not discovery — and it says a
            recall-shaped metric would have looked good from occasion 25 while
            the thing knew nothing.

  P3  **REFUTED.** The walk does not lead at every point. At 100 occasions in
            `together` the partition is ahead (0.1222 against 0.1050), and in
            `alternating` at 400 it is well ahead (0.3141 against 0.2169).

            **The honest reading is that both are at chance until about 400
            occasions and the early ordering is noise.** Chance is 0.1000 and
            every row before 400 sits within 0.05 of it for both mechanisms. The
            walk's advantage is real and arrives late: 0.9665 against 0.6667 at
            the end of `together`.

            So `g38-01`'s result is a large-data effect, which was the refutation
            clause's own wording, and it changes WHEN to use which rather than
            which to use.

  P4  **HELD.** `distractor` is **1.0000** at all eight checkpoints in both
            arms, from 25 occasions onward. It does not start low and rise, and
            it does not fall with experience.

            **So experience cannot fix it either.** That is now five axes —
            three scalar dials, the search budget, and stream length — none of
            which separates the link from the distractor.

  P5  **HELD, and it is the rail that makes the early rows readable.**
            `surfaces` is **18** of a final 111 at 25 occasions and only **98**
            at 1,500. The world is still arriving for half the stream, so the
            early checkpoints are not measuring a mechanism performing badly on
            a complete world — they are measuring an incomplete world.

            **That partly explains the shape but does not explain it away.**
            Between 1,500 and 3,000 the surface count goes 98 to 111, a 13%
            change, while `link` rises 71%. So the late climb is mostly evidence
            accumulating on surfaces already present.


WHAT THIS INVALIDATES, ENUMERATED BEFORE ANYTHING IS BUILT ON TOP
------------------------------------------------------------------
`CLAUDE.md` requires this list to exist as a list, in order of blast radius,
rather than being discovered one surprised result at a time.

**SURVIVES — comparisons between arms at equal stream length.** Every sweep in
g32-g38 ran its arms on the same number of occasions, so which arm is better is
unaffected. That includes `g36-04`'s interleaved-beats-simultaneous, `g38-01`'s
walk-beats-partition, and `g32-01`'s refutation of raw counting.

**BECOMES A LOWER BOUND — every absolute figure.** `0.9665`, `0.9580`, `0.9902`,
`0.9599` and the rest are what the mechanism reached *at 3,000 occasions while
still improving rapidly*. None is what it achieves.

**AT RISK, AND THIS IS THE EXPENSIVE CLASS — every "X does not help".** A
mechanism refuted at 3,000 occasions may be a mechanism whose benefit had not
arrived. `CLAUDE.md`: *"discarding a good idea on an invalid measurement is the
most expensive error available."* The specific entries to re-check are
`g36-06`'s damping exponent and `g35-02`'s churn redundancy.

**NOT AT RISK — `g38-02`'s breadth refutation**, which measures a property of the
data rather than a performance, and `g37-01`/`g37-03`, which are about CLUTRR.

**THE CHEAP FIX IS OBVIOUS AND IS NOT DONE.** FSDD has 3,000 recordings, so 3,000
occasions is one pass over the data. Cycling the stream for several passes is
both trivially available and exactly what a system that learns forever would do.
**Nothing here establishes where the curve actually flattens**, and that is the
next run rather than a caveat.


WHAT THIS DOES NOT SETTLE
-------------------------
**THE QUANTISER IS STILL BATCH**, and the record says so before the results
rather than after. It is fitted on all 4,000 images before the stream begins, so
this is prequential in the LINKING and batch in the PERCEPTION. A genuinely
online system needs an online quantiser, which does not exist.

**One code count and one operating point.** 50 codes, beam 16, depth 1. The
curve's shape almost certainly depends on occasions per CODE rather than per
concept — 50 codes over 3,000 occasions is about 60 each — and that ratio is
held constant here.

**Nothing is distributed.** One process.
