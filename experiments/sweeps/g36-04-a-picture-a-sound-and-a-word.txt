Does a third modality help, and can two senses reach each other?
=================================================================

Script: experiments/g36_04_a_picture_a_sound_and_a_word.py
Status: RUN, local, 2026-07-31, 23.8s. Predictions committed at `d5fec70`
        before the script was executed.

ANSWER: **John's claim is TRUE, and the obvious way of implementing it is the
        one that breaks it.** Two senses that NEVER co-occur reach each other
        through a shared word at **0.9580** — better than two senses that always
        co-occur (0.8853), and better than either sense alone reaches its own
        word. But putting both senses in every occasion **costs 0.23 of image
        linking**, and the reason is measured rather than guessed: the two senses
        evict the word from each other's bounded neighbour list. The word
        survives for **2% of image codes** in `together` against **100%** in
        both other arms (`g36-05`).


IN PLAIN TERMS
--------------
The machine has already learned to connect a written word to pictures of the
thing it names, from nothing but seeing them at the same time. This adds a third
kind of input: **the word said out loud**, three thousand real recordings of
people speaking the digits, six different voices.

Two questions become askable that could not be asked with only two inputs.

**First, John's question.** He suggested that the more different kinds of input
there are, the better this should work — because each extra kind gives another
way to tell two things apart. That sounds obviously right, and there is a reason
to doubt it: the machine keeps only a limited number of connections per thing, so
a third input is also a third competitor for that space. It could help, and it
could crowd out.

**Second, and this is the one that matters more.** You do not usually see a
picture of something at the same moment you hear it said. You see it, or you hear
it. So there is a version of this where the picture and the sound **never once
occur together**, and the only thing joining them is the word that turns up
alongside both.

Can the machine still work out that the picture and the sound are the same thing?
It has never seen them together. If it can, that is the beginning of the answer
to how a concept holds together across senses that never meet. If it cannot, the
thing that has to be added is known and named — but it is not built.

There is also a plain fairness problem being controlled for. Splitting attention
between a picture and a sound means seeing each half as often. So one arm runs at
double the length, to separate *"joining two senses through a word is hard"* from
*"half as much practice is less practice"*.


WHAT IS BEING MEASURED
----------------------
    q_img, q_aud   quantiser purity per modality: share of items sitting in a
                   code whose MAJORITY digit is their own. The seeing and
                   hearing halves, and they are the same for every arm at a
                   given (codes, seed) because the quantisers are computed once
                   and shared -- no arm is scored against a different front end
    link_img       of the IMAGE codes a word's recovered class holds, the share
                   whose majority digit is that word's
    link_aud       the same for AUDIO codes
    cross          THE BRIDGE. Of the audio codes an image code's class holds,
                   the share whose majority digit matches the image code's own
    crossed        how many audio codes an image code reaches at all, per image
                   code. **The companion `cross` needs**: a `cross` of 0.0
                   because everything reached is wrong and one because nothing
                   was reached are different answers, and this line separates
                   them
    classes        mean recovered class size, the collapse guard

**Chance for every purity is the largest class share, about 0.109**, printed by
the run rather than assumed.

`link_*` and `cross` are recall-shaped in the way every metric in this line has
been -- a total collapse puts every surface in every class and scores well.
`classes` is the companion and a collapse shows up there as a number near the
surface count, which is `2*codes + 11`.


MEASURED ON
-----------
Local, single process, Python 3.14. numpy for the quantiser and the FFT only;
both task readers are dependency-free.

    images         4,000   MNIST training prefix, tools/fetch_mnist.py
    recordings     3,000   FSDD, six speakers, 8 kHz mono, tools/fetch_fsdd.py
                           CC BY-SA 4.0, Zohar Jackson et al. Gitignored, not
                           redistributed
    occasions      3,000   one per recording, paired with an image of the same
                           digit taken ROUND-ROBIN from that digit's pool, so
                           every image is used about equally and no pairing is
                           a random draw
    words             10   one token per digit, shared between modalities
    noise              2   OTHER WORDS per occasion, matching g36-01
    distractors        1   present every occasion, carried from g32-01
    image features raw 28x28 greyscale, unit length
    audio features 8 time segments x 16 frequency bands, log energy, unit
                           length. Segments are PROPORTIONAL to recording
                           length, so speaking rate is discarded and spectral
                           shape is kept
    statistic  conditional, the C1-safe one
    bound      derived, `g33-04`'s winner
    seeds      0, 1, 2

Swept: `codes` in {20, 50, 100} -- matching g36-01 so the `image+word` arm is
directly comparable against it -- crossed with five arms.

**BOTH front ends are deliberately weak and BORROWED.** `DECISIONS.md` §1
records John's ruling that a borrowed feature space is acceptable and possibly
preferred. Raw pixels and a crude spectrogram are the cheapest honest choices,
and the point is to measure the LINKING against front ends whose quality is known
and reported.


A NOTE ON WHICH NUMBERS WERE ALREADY KNOWN
-------------------------------------------
`CLAUDE.md` requires this, because a prediction written after a control has run
is a summary rather than a commitment.

**The audio quantiser purity was probed before these predictions were written**,
to check the front end could fire at all -- a run where it sat at chance would
make every downstream number unreadable. Over a 1,500-recording sample at
seed 0: **0.4880 at 20 codes, 0.6640 at 50, 0.8067 at 100**, against chance
0.1087. So P4 below is largely a restatement of something measured, and it is
marked WEAK for that reason.

**The probe also caught a trap that would have invalidated the run.** A first
pass took a PREFIX of the sorted recordings and reported chance 0.20 -- because
FSDD filenames begin with the digit, so the first half of the files are digits 0
to 4 and nothing else. The tell was the chance level, not the purity. `spoken.py`
now says this at `available` and offers `sample`; `mnist.read` takes a prefix
deliberately and says why it is safe there.

Nothing about the LINKING, the bridge, or any arm comparison has been run.


PREDICTIONS
-----------
P1  MORE MODALITIES HELP, WHICH IS JOHN'S CLAIM, AND IT IS TESTED AGAINST THE
    ARM THAT COULD REFUTE IT. `link_img` in `together` is at or above
    `link_img` in `image+word`, at every code count -- the sound adds evidence
    about which pictures belong with which word.
    REFUTED IF `together` is clearly BELOW `image+word` (call it a drop of more
    than 0.05 at two of three code counts). That would mean the third modality
    COMPETES for the derived bound rather than adding to it, extending
    `g36-02`'s finding -- true under a fixed bound -- to the derived one, and it
    would make "add more senses" an active harm rather than a free improvement.

P2  THE STAR SURVIVES CONTACT WITH REAL DATA. In `alternating`, where an image
    code and an audio code share **zero** occasions, `cross` is far above chance
    0.109 -- above **0.50** -- and `crossed` is above 0.5 so the figure is not
    computed over a handful of pairs.
    REFUTED IF `cross` is near chance, or if `crossed` is near zero so there is
    no bridge to score. **That is the hub problem biting on sensory data**:
    `g33-04`'s derived bound would have repaired the star only on symbols this
    project generated, and two senses that never co-occur would need a mechanism
    that does not exist yet.

P3  A DIRECT EDGE BEATS A BRIDGED ONE. `cross` in `together` exceeds `cross` in
    `alternating x2`, which is matched on per-modality exposure, at every code
    count.
    REFUTED IF the bridged version equals or beats the direct one. **That would
    be the most interesting outcome available here**: it would say a word is a
    BETTER route between two senses than co-occurring with them is -- that the
    shared name denoises what direct co-occurrence does not -- and it would
    change what the architecture should reach for first.

P4  **WEAK, and marked so.** Audio is the poorer front end and the linking
    inherits it: `link_aud` is below `link_img` at every code count.
    REFUTED IF `link_aud` matches or beats `link_img`. That is not the trivial
    outcome it looks like: it would mean the linking is limited by something
    other than front-end quality, since the audio quantiser is 0.24 worse at 20
    codes. The quantiser purities are already known -- see above -- so only the
    claim about the LINK is a commitment.

P5  SPLITTING COSTS SOMETHING, AND LESS THAN HALF. `link_img` in `alternating`
    is below `link_img` in `image+word` -- half the exposure -- but by less than
    the gap between `image+word` at 20 codes and at 50, because `g32-02` put the
    threshold at about 16 occasions per concept and even the split arm gives 150
    per digit.
    REFUTED IF `alternating` collapses, which would mean the occasion threshold
    binds far higher on real sensory data than on the synthetic stream.

---------

            prediction set.** `link_img` in `together` against `image+word`:

                20 codes   0.6190  against  0.7986    -0.18
                50 codes   0.6667  against  0.8961    -0.23
               100 codes   0.2265  against  0.4448    -0.22

            The refutation condition was a drop over 0.05 at two of three code
            counts. It is over 0.18 at three of three.

            **So adding a third modality to every occasion is an active harm**,
            and John's claim as literally stated -- more input types that
            CO-OCCUR gives more differentiation -- is false on real sensory data
            under the derived bound. `g36-02` found the same claim false under a
            fixed bound; it is now false under both.

            **The mechanism was not left as a guess.** `g36-05` measures it: in
            `together` the word survives the derived bound for **2%** of image
            codes against **100%** in the other two arms, its mean rank falls
            from 1.02 to 6.60, and of the 1.78 partners the cliff keeps, **1.76
            are audio codes.** A picture and a sound that always arrive together
            become each other's strongest partner and evict the word.

  P2  **HELD, and it is the finding.** In `alternating`, where an image code and
            an audio code share **zero** occasions, `cross` is 0.8297 and 0.8011
            at 20 and 50 codes against chance 0.1000, with `crossed` at 0.90 and
            3.62 so the figure is not computed over a handful of pairs.

            **Two senses that never once co-occur reach each other through a
            shared word.** The star survives contact with real sensory data, and
            `g33-04`'s derived bound transfers off the synthetic stream.

  P3  **REFUTED BACKWARDS, which this file named as the most interesting outcome
            available.** `cross` in `together` against `alternating x2`, matched
            on per-modality exposure:

                20 codes   0.6568  against  0.8105
                50 codes   0.8853  against  0.9580
               100 codes   0.4531  against  0.6899

            **The bridged route beats the direct one, every time.** A shared name
            is a better route between two senses than co-occurring with them is.

            That is not mysterious once P1's mechanism is in hand: a direct edge
            is bought with the hub connection, because the bound is a fixed
            budget. The word is a cleaner signal about identity than a
            simultaneous sensation is, and paying for the sensation costs the
            word.

  P4  **REFUTED.** `link_aud` matches or beats `link_img` wherever both are
            present: 0.8619 against 0.7848 at 20 codes `alternating`, 0.9825
            against 0.9850 at 50 `alternating x2`, and the best single link in
            the table is `audio+word` at **0.9902** -- from the front end that is
            0.185 WORSE at quantising.

            So **the linking is not limited by front-end quality** over this
            range. That is the substantive half: a 0.6449 quantiser supports a
            0.9902 link, so effort spent on better perception would not have
            bought better grounding here. `g36-01` reached the same conclusion
            from the other side, measuring the linking as within five points of
            what its front end permitted.

  P5  **REFUTED, backwards again.** `alternating` does not fall below
            `image+word` by a small amount; at 50 codes `alternating x2` reaches
            **0.9850 against 0.8961**, and at 100 codes **0.7058 against
            0.4448**. Splitting a stream across two senses and then giving it the
            same per-sense exposure is BETTER than never splitting it.

            Half the exposure does cost something -- `alternating` at 50 codes is
            0.8476 against `alternating x2`'s 0.9850 -- so the exposure axis is
            real. It is simply smaller than the benefit of having a second sense
            at all.


WHAT THIS CHANGES
-----------------
**John's intuition is right about the WORLD and wrong about the mechanism.** More
senses genuinely does buy more differentiation -- 0.9850 against 0.8961 is the
measurement of it. But the benefit arrives when the senses are *interleaved*, and
inverts when they are *simultaneous*, and nothing in the intuition predicted that
sign flip.

**The good news is that interleaved is what reality looks like.** You rarely see
a thing at the exact moment you hear its name spoken by someone else and hear the
thing itself. `alternating` is the realistic stream and `together` is the
artificial one.

**And it says not to build the obvious thing.** The natural response to the hub
problem is a mechanism letting two spokes notice they share a hub and connect
directly. This measures that arrangement -- `together` IS spokes directly
connected -- and it is worse in every cell. The walk already bridges them
transitively, at 0.9580.


WHAT THIS DOES NOT SETTLE
-------------------------
**Ten concepts, and they are digits.** Unchanged from `g36-01`, and the star here
has ten hubs with two spokes each. A real hub has many more spokes and `g33-02`
measured that the degree is what breaks a fixed bound.

**`together` is the EXTREME of simultaneity** -- both senses in 100% of
occasions. The interesting axis is the one between: what share of occasions may
carry both senses before the eviction starts? That is one parameter and is not
swept here.

**G7 is still not passed**, for `g36-01`'s reason: no arm introduces a concept
through one modality and queries it through another. Every arm here has the word
present throughout.

**Nothing is distributed.** One process. `g35-01` and `g35-03` are the container
path.
