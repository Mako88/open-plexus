Does the refusal survive a thing present ALMOST always?
========================================================

Script: experiments/g39_06_what_about_a_thing_present_almost_always.py
Status: RUN, local, 2026-07-31, 23.1s. Predictions committed at `253418c`
        before the script was executed.

ANSWER: **Partial presence does nothing at all, exactly as the arithmetic said
        — and the CONFOUND arm is a coin on edge that a pooled mean hid.**

        Independent presence from 1.0 down to 0.5 leaves the distractor's score
        at 0.2805-0.2808 and the margin at 0.4488-0.4491. The `p` cancels, so
        `g39-05`'s caveat dissolves rather than being answered.

        **The correlated arm pooled over ten words reads margin 0.4494 and looks
        fine. Scored on the digit it actually favours it reads 0.0096** —
        weakest true partner 0.6307 against the confound's 0.6211. A 47-fold
        collapse, invisible in the pooled row.

        It is still not admitted, at rank 7.7 against a want of 4.7. **But it is
        refused by a hair rather than by a margin**, and that is the honest
        boundary of everything `g39-04` and `g39-05` established.


IN PLAIN TERMS
--------------
The last run showed the machine reliably ignoring a hum that is present in every
single moment. But it named its own weak spot: something present in EVERY moment
is the easiest case to dismiss. A lamp is in most rooms, not all of them, and
something present ninety percent of the time is a harder problem.

So this turns the dial down and watches.

There is a reason to think nothing will happen, and it is worth stating before
the run because it is a real prediction rather than a hope. The machine judges a
connection by a proportion -- how often these two appear together, divided by how
often that one appears at all. If the lamp is switched on at random for nine
tenths of moments, then BOTH of those numbers drop by a tenth, and the proportion
is unchanged. The dial should do nothing.

If that turns out to be wrong, then the explanation we just published for why any
of this works is also wrong, because it is the same piece of arithmetic read from
the other side. So this is a test of the account, not just of the mechanism.

**And there is a case the arithmetic explicitly does not cover**, included here
so the boundary is on the record rather than discovered later.

The cancellation assumes the lamp is switched on without regard to what is being
shown. Something that turns up more often around ONE thing is not background at
all -- it genuinely is associated with that thing, and no amount of counting can
say otherwise, because from the data there is no difference between "associated
with" and "genuinely part of". That arm is here to show where the wall is. **It
is expected to fail, and failing is the correct behaviour.**


WHAT IS BEING MEASURED
----------------------
    rank           where the distractor sits in a word's full ranking
    want           how many image codes genuinely belong to that word
    weakest true   the lowest-scoring genuine partner
    distractor     the distractor's own score
    margin         weakest true minus distractor
    admitted       whether it entered the top-`want`. `g32-01`'s falsifier

Same columns as `g39-05` so the two tables read side by side.


MEASURED ON
-----------
Local, one process. Stream reproduced from `g39-05` at half the passes.

    images       4,000   MNIST prefix
    recordings   3,000   FSDD, six speakers
    occasions   12,000   four passes. `g39-02` put the plateau at 12,000
    codes           50
    statistic  conditional, read FORWARD from the word
    seeds      0, 1, 2

Swept: distractor `presence` in {0.5, 0.7, 0.9, 0.95, 1.0}, drawn independently
per occasion, plus one `correlated` arm present on every occasion of digit 3 and
10% elsewhere.


PREDICTIONS
-----------
P1  **PRESENCE DOES NOTHING, AND THE ARITHMETIC SAYS WHY.** `admitted` is 0.0000
    at every independent presence from 1.0 down to 0.5, and the distractor's own
    score varies by less than 0.05 across them, because `p` cancels between the
    numerator and the denominator.
    REFUTED IF the score moves with presence. **Then the published account of
    why `forward` works is wrong**, since it rests on the same cancellation, and
    `g39-04` would need re-examining rather than extending.

P2  THE MARGIN IS THEREFORE ALSO FLAT. `margin` stays near `g39-05`'s 0.4464 at
    every independent presence.
    REFUTED IF it narrows as presence falls, which would mean partial presence
    is a real threat and `g39-05`'s caveat was correct.

P3  **THE CORRELATED ARM IS ADMITTED, and that is the RIGHT answer rather than a
    failure.** `admitted` is above 0.05 for it.
    REFUTED IF it is refused. That would be surprising and would need explaining
    -- a surface genuinely more common around one concept SHOULD score highly
    for that concept, and a mechanism refusing it would be refusing real
    evidence.

P4  THE CORRELATED ARM HURTS ONLY THE DIGIT IT FAVOURS. Since `admitted` is
    averaged over ten words and only digit 3 is favoured, it lands near 0.1
    rather than near 1.0.
    REFUTED IF it is admitted for most words, which would mean the correlation
    leaks across concepts and the arm is not testing what it claims.

AND THE PROBE HAD THE DEFECT ITS OWN PREDECESSOR WARNED ABOUT
---------------------------------------------------------------
The `correlated` row was FIRST reported pooled over all ten words, where it reads
margin **0.4494** and looks indistinguishable from the independent arms. The
correlation touches one digit, so nine irrelevant words diluted it away.

**`g39-05`'s own "what this does not settle" says exactly this**, one run
earlier: *"a single bad word could sit far lower without moving it."* The probe
testing that caveat was built with the caveat in it.

It was caught by re-reading that section rather than by any check, and the
`-> digit 3` row exists because of it. **A warning one run old did not prevent
the repeat** — the same shape as `g38-03` dropping a companion column the day
after `g38-01` explained why it mattered.


SCORECARD
---------
  P1  **HELD, and it confirms the published account rather than just the
            mechanism.** The distractor's score is 0.2808, 0.2806, 0.2805,
            0.2805, 0.2806 across presences from 0.5 to 1.0 — flat to three
            decimals, against a predicted tolerance of 0.05.

            `p` appears in both the numerator and the denominator and cancels.
            **Since `g39-04`'s explanation of why `forward` works is the same
            cancellation read from the other side, this is evidence for the
            account and not only for the result.**

  P2  **HELD.** `margin` is 0.4488 to 0.4491 across independent presences,
            against `g39-05`'s 0.4464 at a different stream length. Partial
            presence is not a threat.

  P3  **REFUTED AS WRITTEN, AND THE SPIRIT IS WHAT MATTERS.** The correlated arm
            was predicted to be admitted. It is not — `admitted` 0.0000, rank
            **7.7** against want **4.7**.

            **But the margin collapses from 0.4490 to 0.0096**, a factor of 47.
            The weakest true partner scores 0.6307 and the confound 0.6211.
            **It is outside the top-`want` by a hair, not by a margin.**

            The arithmetic says why it survives at all: this confound is present
            on 100% of digit 3's occasions and 10% elsewhere, so it is about 63%
            specific to that concept, while an image code of digit 3 is
            essentially 100% specific. **A confound has to be MORE concentrated
            on a concept than the concept's own surfaces are before it displaces
            them** — a high bar, and the reason the refusal holds here.

            **A stronger confound would cross it**, and nothing here establishes
            where. That is the untested boundary.

  P4  **REFUTED, and unreadable as originally computed.** The pooled `admitted`
            was predicted near 0.1 and is 0.0000, because the confound is not
            admitted even for the digit it favours. The prediction assumed it
            would be, which P3 already covers.


WHAT THIS CHANGES
-----------------
**`g39-05`'s caveat is answered and dissolved.** A thing present almost always is
no harder than a thing present always, because the statistic is a ratio and
partial presence cancels out of it. A lamp in most rooms is refused exactly as a
lamp in all rooms is.

**The real boundary is CORRELATION, not partial presence**, and it is much
closer than any previous number suggested. At 63% concept-specificity the margin
is 0.0096. **Every headline in this line — 0.9867, the 0.4464 margin, "the
distractor is refused" — is measured against distractors that are uncorrelated
with the concept.**

**And no statistic over co-occurrence can fix that**, which is not a defect of
`forward`: a surface genuinely more common around one concept IS evidence about
that concept, and the data contains no signal distinguishing "spuriously
correlated" from "actually part of it". `g32-01` names intervention as the only
escape and this project has not tested it.


WHAT THIS DOES NOT SETTLE
-------------------------
**Where the confound boundary actually is.** One correlation strength was tried,
100%/10%. The margin at that point is 0.0096, so the crossing is very close and
nothing locates it.

**One code count.** `want` is about 5 because 50 codes over 10 digits gives 5
each. A confound competes against however many true partners there are, so the
bar moves with the code count.

**Intervention is untested and is the only named escape.** Nothing in this
project has run it.
