Can a softer denominator save the word without letting the distractor back in?
===============================================================================

Script: experiments/g36_06_can_a_softer_denominator_save_the_word.py
Status: RUN, local, 2026-07-31, 25.5s. Predictions committed at `05d4dd4`
        before the script was executed.

ANSWER: **The diagnosis is right and the fix does not work.** Softening the
        denominator restores the word's PRESENCE in a neighbour list — 0.0200 to
        0.9400 — and destroys the MUTUAL structure the walk runs on, so
        end-to-end linking goes to **0.0000**. `alpha = 1` remains the best value
        in both arms. Three of five predictions refuted, and the most useful
        refutation is that the low end fails by FRAGMENTING rather than by
        merging, which is the opposite of what was predicted.


IN PLAIN TERMS
--------------
When the machine decides which things belong together, it does not just count how
often two things turn up at the same time. It divides that count by how common
the second thing is on its own — otherwise something that is *always* around wins
every comparison by being always around.

That correction was necessary and it was measured: without it, a permanent piece
of background noise beats every real connection.

But it has a cost that only showed up once there were three kinds of input.
There are ten words and a hundred codes for pictures and sounds, so **each word
turns up about fourteen times as often as any single picture code** — not because
it matters less, but because ten things are sharing the work of a hundred. The
correction reads that commonness as unimportance and pushes the word out.

John spotted this and asked the obvious question: **can we turn the correction
down a bit?**

So this turns it into a dial. All the way up is what we have now. All the way
down is plain counting, which we already know fails. The question is whether
anywhere in between keeps the word *and* keeps the background noise out.

**It is entirely possible the answer is no.** The two failures may sit at
opposite ends with no gap between them, and if so that is worth knowing plainly,
because it says the fix has to be something other than tuning this number.


THE ASYMMETRY THAT PROMPTED THIS, MEASURED
-------------------------------------------
Mean times present, on this stream, arm `together`, 50 codes, seed 0:

    image code    50 of them    present   60.0 times on average
    audio code    50 of them    present   60.0
    word          10 of them    present  845.4

Ten words carry the occasions that fifty codes split between them, so a word is
present about **fourteen times** as often as any single code. `conditional`
divides by the candidate's own count, which turns that into a fourteen-fold
handicap applied to the word for being shared across fewer TYPES rather than for
being less informative.

**Provenance:** measured by a probe on this same stream before this sweep was
written, not by this script. It is the motivating observation rather than a
result, and it is recorded here so anything quoting it has one place to point at.

For one image code the concrete case is: the word co-occurs with it in **50** of
its **65** appearances — 77% — and still scores below an audio code that shares
far fewer.


WHAT IS BEING MEASURED
----------------------
    word          share of image codes whose OWN word survives the derived
                  bound. `g36-05`'s quantity, and the thing the dial must move
    distractor    share of image codes admitting the ever-present distractor.
                  `g32-01`'s falsifier, and the thing it must not break
    link_img      the end-to-end consequence, comparable with `g36-04`
    cross         the cross-sensory bridge, comparable with `g36-04`
    crossed       audio codes reached per image code -- the companion `cross`
                  needs, since 0.0 from reaching nothing and 0.0 from reaching
                  only wrong things are different answers
    classes       mean recovered class size. The collapse guard, and it matters
                  MORE here than usual: a low alpha is expected to merge things,
                  and a merged graph scores well on every recall-shaped column

**Both failure modes are watched in the same run**, which is the design point. A
sweep that only watched `word` would report alpha 0 as a triumph while the
distractor walked back in, and `g32-01`'s calibration is exactly that shape --
`captured` would have called a distractor a rounding error while it destroyed a
third of the recovery.

PREDICTIONS
-----------
P1  THE TWO FAILURES ARE MONOTONE AND OPPOSITE. In `together`, `word` falls as
    alpha rises and `distractor` falls as alpha rises -- both moving the same way
    with the dial, which is what makes it a trade rather than a tuning problem.
    REFUTED IF either is non-monotone, which would mean the exponent is not
    acting the way the arithmetic says and something else is moving.

P2  **THE HEADLINE, AND IT IS WRITTEN TO FAIL.** No alpha achieves both. There is
    no value with `word` above 0.80 and `distractor` below 0.05.
    REFUTED IF some alpha does both -- **which would make John's fix correct as
    stated and would be the best available outcome here.** It would mean the
    eviction is a tuning error rather than a structural conflict, and the
    `together` arrangement stops being one to avoid.

P3  A LOW ALPHA MERGES THINGS. `classes` at alpha 0.0 is at least twice its value
    at alpha 1.0, so any improvement in `link_img` or `cross` at the low end has
    to be read against a graph that is collapsing.
    REFUTED IF `classes` is flat across alpha. Then a low alpha is not buying its
    numbers with over-merging and the recall-shaped columns can be read directly.

P4  **JOHN'S DIAGNOSIS IS NECESSARY BUT NOT SUFFICIENT, and this is the arm that
    tests it.** In `alternating`, `word` stays above 0.80 at EVERY alpha
    including 1.0 -- because the word is just as common there, so if volume
    asymmetry alone caused the eviction it would happen there too. It does not:
    what `together` adds is a rival that is both well-correlated and rare.
    REFUTED IF `alternating` also loses the word at high alpha. Then the
    asymmetry alone is the whole cause, John's account is complete as stated, and
    the rival plays no part.

P5  THE 0.5 ROW REPRODUCES `weighted`. `link_img` at alpha 0.5 in `together`
    matches what `frequency_weighted` would give, because the family reproduces
    it exactly -- asserted in `tests/test_damped.py` against that implementation.
    This is a WIRING check rather than a scientific claim, and it is here because
    a mutation that made every alpha compute `conditional` **survived the first
    version of those tests**: two orderings coincided on the fixture. A flat row
    across alpha is the symptom to watch for.

SCORECARD
---------
  P1  **REFUTED on `word`, held on `distractor`.** `distractor` falls
            monotonically with alpha in both arms — 1.0000, 0.9333, 0.1867,
            0.0000, 0.0000 — exactly as the arithmetic says.

            `word` in `together` does NOT: **0.8200, 0.9467, 0.9400, 0.3600,
            0.0200.** It RISES from alpha 0 to 0.25 and then falls off a cliff
            between 0.5 and 0.75. So the dial is not a trade with two clean ends;
            the low end has its own failure and it is not the one predicted.

  P2  **REFUTED AS WRITTEN, AND THE REFUTATION IS WHY THE CRITERION WAS WRONG.**
            The stated condition was `word` above 0.80 with `distractor` below
            0.05. **`alternating` at alpha 0.75 meets it exactly** — 0.9865 and
            0.0135 — and so, nearly, does alpha 1.00.

            **And at that cell `link_img` is 0.1385 with `classes` at 35.69**,
            which is a collapsed graph. The two-metric criterion is satisfied
            while the task fails.

            That is `g32-01`'s calibration arriving in this file's own
            predictions: `captured` would have called a distractor a rounding
            error while it destroyed a third of the recovery, and here a
            word-survival criterion calls a collapse a success. **The criterion
            should have carried `classes` in it, and it was written without.**

            **On the question John actually asked, the answer is NO.** No alpha
            beats 1.0 end to end in either arm. `together` peaks at alpha 1.00
            (0.6667) and `alternating` peaks at alpha 1.00 (0.8476). Softening
            the denominator does not recover the word; it recovers the word's
            presence in a list while removing what made the list useful.

  P3  **REFUTED, BACKWARDS, and this is the most informative line in the run.**
            `classes` at alpha 0.0 is **1.99**, against **4.26** at alpha 1.0 in
            `together` and **8.89** in `alternating`. It was predicted to be at
            least twice as large; it is less than half.

            **The low end fails by FRAGMENTING, not by merging.** The reason is
            mutuality. At alpha 0 every surface ranks the ever-present distractor
            first, so the bound is spent on it and almost no pair is mutual — the
            graph is singletons. Over-merging was the intuitive failure mode of a
            weak correction and it is not what happens.

            **So `word` and `link_img` are not two views of one thing.** A word
            can be in an image code's list (0.9400) while no image code is in any
            word's list, because a hub can be mutual with only as many spokes as
            its own bound admits. That is the hub problem again, from the third
            direction this week.

  P4  **HELD, and it settles John's account precisely.** In `alternating` the
            word survives at **every** alpha: 0.8389, 0.9664, 0.9865, 0.9865,
            0.9797 — including 1.00, where `together` reads 0.0200.

            A word is just as common in `alternating` as in `together`. So volume
            asymmetry alone does NOT evict it. **His diagnosis of the mechanism
            is correct and is necessary; it is not sufficient.** What `together`
            adds is a rival that is both well-correlated with the code and RARE,
            and the denominator only punishes the word when there is such a rival
            to be punished in favour of.

  P5  HELD as a wiring check. The alpha column is not flat — every quantity moves
            across it — so the exponent reaches the score. This was worth keeping
            because a mutation making every alpha compute `conditional` survived
            the first version of `tests/test_damped.py`, where two orderings
            coincided on the fixture.


WHAT THIS CHANGES
-----------------
**`conditional` stays, and the reason for keeping it is now stronger than the
reason it was chosen.** It was adopted because it kills a persistent distractor
at no cost (`g32-01`) and is C1-safe. It now also has the only end-to-end
numbers in this table.

**`damped` stays as a switched-off alternative**, per `CLAUDE.md` 14c: it carries
why it lost, with this record's number, and it costs a config field. Refutations
expire, and the one condition that would revive it is named below.

**The thing to stop doing is looking for a scalar fix to the hub problem.** Three
runs now have found the same wall from three directions — `g33-02`'s global `k`,
`g36-05`'s eviction, and this run's fragmentation. All three are the same fact:
**a bound is a budget, and a hub's budget is what limits how many spokes it can
be MUTUAL with.** No choice of statistic changes the size of the budget.

**What that points at instead** is the bound itself being per-DIRECTION or
per-KIND rather than one number per surface — a word keeping more partners than a
picture does, because it is a word. That is untried, it is not a scalar, and it
is the first mechanism proposal in this line that the measurements actually
motivate rather than merely permit.


WHAT THIS DOES NOT SETTLE
-------------------------
**One code count, pinned at 50 from `g36-04`.** The bound's budget and the number
of codes per digit are related, and this sweep holds the second fixed. `g33-04`'s
lesson about carried constants applies: 50 was the middle of `g36-04`'s
{20, 50, 100} and is not known to be right for this question.

**Alpha above 1.0 was not tried.** The family permits it and nothing here
explores it. The arithmetic suggests it would punish the word harder, so it is
predicted to be worse rather than shown to be — say so if it is ever quoted.

**The revival condition for `damped`:** a stream where no distractor is present
on every occasion. Every number above is measured with one, because that is
`g32-01`'s falsifier condition, and it is what makes the low end of the axis fail
immediately. On a clean stream the trade might have an interior. Untested.
