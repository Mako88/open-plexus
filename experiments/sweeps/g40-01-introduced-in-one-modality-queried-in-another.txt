GATE G7 — a concept introduced in one modality, queried in another
===================================================================

Script: experiments/g40_01_introduced_in_one_modality_queried_in_another.py
Status: RUN, local, 2026-07-31, 22.5s. Predictions committed at `3bb5005`
        before the script was executed.

ANSWER: **G7 PASSES, and its falsifier did not fire.** From an image code, the
        walk reaches audio codes of the same digit at **0.8464** against chance
        **0.10** — with the two modalities sharing ZERO occasions and the word
        as the only route.

        **Order is free and is now demonstrated rather than assumed**: the same
        occasions shuffled produce a bit-identical result, 0.8464 and 0.9000 to
        four decimals.

        **The exposure threshold is HIGH and that is the honest limitation.**
        `cross` collapses from 0.8437 at a 25% sound share to **0.2623** at 10%
        and to chance below that. So the second modality needs roughly a
        QUARTER of the stream — 300 occasions per digit — not the handful
        predicted. P3 refuted.


IN PLAIN TERMS
--------------
This is the gate the whole grounding line was built for, and it has never been
run.

The question: can the machine learn what something is from one sense, and then
recognise it through a different sense it barely encountered?

Everything measured so far has stopped short of that in one specific way. The
word has been present in every single moment, alongside both the picture and the
sound. So what was really tested was whether three things that always arrive
together can be recognised as one thing. That is a fair question and it was
answered, but it is not this one.

Here the picture and the sound **never once occur together**, in any arm. The
only thing joining them is the word — and in most arms the sound turns up only at
the very end, and only briefly.

**One thing has to be said plainly, because it makes the gate easier than it
sounds.** This machine counts what turns up with what, and a count does not care
what order things arrived in. Showing all the pictures first and all the sounds
afterwards produces exactly the same tally as mixing them. So the *order* part of
this gate cannot fail — not because the machine is clever, but because the
question does not bite on a tally.

Claiming a pass on that basis would be passing a test by construction. So the
real question is the one underneath: **how little of the second sense is enough?**

That is a genuine threshold, it is not free, and it is what gets swept. If it
takes as much of the sound as of the picture, the answer is unimpressive. If a
handful of examples is enough, that is a real result about learning from very
little.


WHAT IS BEING MEASURED
----------------------
    cross      **the gate.** From an IMAGE code, does the walk reach an AUDIO
               code of the same digit? They share zero occasions in every arm
    crossed    how many audio codes an image code reaches at all. The companion
               `cross` needs -- reaching nothing and reaching wrong things are
               different answers, and this line has been caught by that twice
    word_aud   from the WORD, does it reach the right audio codes? The half of
               the route that has to work first
    heard      how many audio codes were scored at all

**Chance is 0.10.** A concept has about 5 image codes and 5 audio codes.


MEASURED ON
-----------
Local, one process.

    images       4,000   MNIST prefix
    recordings   3,000   FSDD, six speakers
    occasions   12,000   four passes, re-paired
    codes           50
    statistic  conditional read FORWARD -- `g39-04`'s winner
    beam            16   `g38-03`'s operating point
    depth            2   **required here**: an image code reaches a sound only
                         through the word, which is two hops. `g38-03` found
                         depth 2 unnecessary for a one-hop query and this is not
                         one
    seeds      0, 1, 2

Arms: `phased` at sound-shares {0.01, 0.02, 0.05, 0.10, 0.25, 0.50}, plus
`interleaved` at 0.50 as the control.

**In `phased` the sound occasions are all at the END of the stream.** In
`interleaved` they are mixed throughout. The tally cannot tell them apart, which
is exactly what P1 exists to demonstrate rather than assume.


PREDICTIONS
-----------
P1  **ORDER IS FREE, AND THIS DEMONSTRATES IT RATHER THAN ASSUMING IT.**
    `phased` at 0.50 matches `interleaved` at 0.50 on every column, within 0.02.
    REFUTED IF they differ. **That would mean something in the pipeline is
    order-sensitive when the tally is not** -- most likely the quantiser or the
    re-pairing cursor -- and every result in this line that assumed order does
    not matter would need re-checking.

P2  **THE GATE PASSES AT FAIR EXPOSURE.** `cross` at share 0.50 is far above
    chance 0.10 -- above 0.70 -- with `crossed` above 1.0 so it is not computed
    over a handful.
    REFUTED IF it is near chance. **That is G7's falsifier firing**, and
    `GOALS.md` §1.2b's answer to symbol grounding fails on the first run that
    actually asks it.

P3  **THE THRESHOLD IS LOW, AND THIS IS THE REAL CONTENT.** `cross` stays above
    0.70 down to a share of 0.05 -- 600 sound occasions, 60 per digit.
    REFUTED IF it collapses above that. Then the second modality needs
    comparable exposure to the first, and *"introduced in one, queried in
    another"* is true only in a weak sense.

P4  IT BREAKS SOMEWHERE. At share 0.01 -- 120 occasions, 12 per digit -- `cross`
    is below 0.70, because `g32-02` put the concept threshold at about 16
    occasions and 12 is under it.
    REFUTED IF 0.01 still works, which would be a stronger result than predicted
    and would say the threshold is below anything this grid resolves.

P5  `word_aud` LEADS `cross`. The word reaches the sounds before an image code
    can, because that is one hop and the cross-modal route is two.
    REFUTED IF `cross` exceeds `word_aud` anywhere -- the route cannot be better
    than its own first leg, so that would indicate a scoring defect rather than
    a finding.

P5 is the rail. If it fails, the two-hop path is not doing what the columns say
and P2-P4 are unreadable.

A DESIGN DEFECT CAUGHT AND FIXED BEFORE ANYTHING WAS CONCLUDED
----------------------------------------------------------------
The first version of this run reported `interleaved` at cross **0.8029** against
`phased`'s 0.8464 — a gap of 0.0435 that would have REFUTED P1 and implied
something in the pipeline was order-sensitive.

**It was not a finding. The two arms were observing different data.** The arm
decided whether an occasion carried the sound inline — deterministically in
`phased` and by a coin in `interleaved` — so they got different image/audio
pairings and, because the coin consumed randomness, different noise draws too.

The fix is to build the occasion list ONCE and then either observe it in order or
shuffle it, so the multiset is identical and only the order differs. With that,
the two arms agree to four decimals.

**A comparison between two orderings has to hold the data fixed**, and deciding
the split inline does not. Recorded because the wrong version produced a
plausible number and a plausible story.


SCORECARD
---------
  P1  **HELD, after the fix above, and it is now a demonstration rather than an
            assumption.** The same occasions shuffled give cross **0.8464** and
            word_aud **0.9000** — bit-identical to the phased order.

            So a co-occurrence tally genuinely does not care when things
            arrived, and the ORDER half of G7 is free for this mechanism. Saying
            that out loud is what stops this run being a gate passed by
            construction.

  P2  **HELD, AND IT IS THE GATE.** `cross` is **0.8464** at fair exposure
            against chance 0.10, with `crossed` at **5.03** audio codes per
            image code, so it is not computed over a handful.

            **An image code and an audio code share zero occasions in every arm.**
            The machine gets from a picture to a sound it has never encountered
            alongside it, through a word that co-occurred with both at different
            times.

            **G7's falsifier — *"the same concept cannot be reached from two
            modalities under these constraints"* — did not fire.**

  P3  **REFUTED, and this is the honest limitation of the pass.** `cross` was
            predicted above 0.70 down to a 5% share. It reads **0.1260** there,
            which is chance.

            The collapse is between 25% and 10%:

                share 0.25   300 per digit   cross 0.8437
                share 0.10   120 per digit   cross 0.2623
                share 0.05    60 per digit   cross 0.1260

            **So the second modality needs roughly a quarter of the stream**,
            not a handful of examples. *"Introduced in one modality, queried in
            another"* is true in the sense the gate asks, and it is NOT true in
            the stronger sense of learning a new sense from brief exposure.

            The threshold is also far above `g32-02`'s ~16 occasions per
            concept, which was measured for a single modality. **A cross-modal
            link costs an order of magnitude more exposure than a within-modal
            one**, and that comparison is new.

  P4  **HELD.** At a 1% share — 12 occasions per digit — `cross` is **0.1154**,
            which is chance. The mechanism does break, and where predicted.

  P5  **HELD where it is readable.** `word_aud` leads `cross` at every share
            from 0.10 upward: 0.2729/0.2623, 0.8933/0.8437, 0.9000/0.8464. The
            two-hop route is never better than its own first leg.

            At shares 0.02 and 0.05 `cross` marginally exceeds `word_aud`
            (0.0902/0.0847 and 0.1260/0.1248), **but both sit at chance there**,
            so the ordering is noise rather than the defect P5 was written to
            catch. The rail holds in the interpretable region and says nothing
            outside it.


WHAT THIS CHANGES
-----------------
**G7 is passed and `GOALS.md` §4 is updated**, which is the only place a gate
verdict is written.

**And the pass is narrower than the gate's wording suggests**, which the record
says here so nothing downstream over-reads it. What is demonstrated is that a
concept's two senses need never co-occur. What is NOT demonstrated is learning a
modality from brief exposure — that needs a quarter of the stream.

**A cross-modal link is about an order of magnitude dearer than a within-modal
one** — 300 occasions per digit against `g32-02`'s ~16. That is a new quantity
and it is the number to design against.


WHAT THIS DOES NOT SETTLE
-------------------------
**The threshold is bracketed, not located.** It falls between a 10% and a 25%
share and this grid has no point between them.

**One code count, one beam, one depth.** 50, 16, 2. `want` is about 5 audio
codes per digit because 50 codes over 10 digits gives 5 each, so the threshold
may be a statement about occasions per CODE rather than per digit.

**The quantiser is still batch**, fitted on all the data before the stream
begins. So the sound's CODES existed before the sound was ever heard, and a
genuinely online system would have to form them from the brief exposure too.
**That is the part of "learning a new sense late" this does not test at all.**
