IN PLAIN TERMS
--------------
The previous run found that the machine was still learning fast when it ran out
of data. Everything we have reported is therefore a floor rather than a result --
we stopped watching while it was still improving.

So this gives it eight times as much experience and watches where it settles.

There is a trap in doing that, and it is worth stating because the obvious
version does nothing at all. You cannot simply play the same three thousand
moments over again. The machine judges connections by proportion -- how often
these two appear together, relative to how often that one appears at all -- and
playing the same tape twice doubles both numbers and changes no proportion.
Every score would come out identical and the flat line would be an artefact of
the arithmetic rather than a finding about learning.

So instead the same underlying material is recombined. The same three thousand
recordings and the same pictures, but each pass pairs them up differently and the
background chatter is redrawn. That is genuinely new experience, and it is also
the honest model of a real learner: you do not meet new dogs forever, you meet
the same dogs in new situations.

The thing being watched hardest is the one failure nothing has fixed. There is an
ever-present hum that the machine wrongly treats as meaningful, and it has done
so at every setting of every dial we have tried, and at every point in the
stream. More experience is the last cheap thing left to try on it.


WHAT IS BEING MEASURED
----------------------
The same four quantities as `g39-01`, scored by the SAME imported function, at
3,000 / 6,000 / 12,000 / 24,000 occasions.

    link         precision over the top-`k` image codes a word reaches
    covered      how many of the codes it SHOULD find it had a chance to score.
                 `link` is not readable without it
    partition    the incumbent `equivalence_classes`, on the same curve
    distractor   share of words admitting the ever-present distractor
    surfaces     the rail -- how much of the world exists yet

**The 3,000 column must reproduce `g39-01`'s `together` row**, which is what
makes the rest of the table trustworthy. It will not match to four decimals,
because the pairing cursor and the noise draw advance differently once passes are
possible, and that is stated in advance rather than explained afterwards.

PREDICTIONS
-----------
P1  IT FLATTENS BY 12,000. `link` moves less than 0.03 between 12,000 and 24,000.
    REFUTED IF it is still climbing by more than 0.03 over that doubling. Then
    the plateau is further out than eight passes reaches, and stream length has
    to become a swept axis in this line rather than a constant -- which is a
    larger change to how everything here is measured.

P2  IT LANDS ABOVE 0.98. With eight times the evidence, `link` exceeds the
    0.9665 reported at 3,000.
    REFUTED IF it falls. A DECLINE would mean extra pairings are adding
    confusion rather than evidence -- each pass pairs a recording with a
    different image, so more passes make the audio-to-image association broader,
    and that could dilute rather than sharpen.

P3  THE PARTITION CLOSES SOME OF THE GAP. `partition` improves more than `link`
    does over the extension, because it starts further from its ceiling --
    0.6667 against 0.9665.
    REFUTED IF the gap widens. That would strengthen `g38-01`'s conclusion
    rather than qualify it.

P4  **THE ONE WORTH RUNNING FOR.** `distractor` stays at 1.0000 at every
    checkpoint.
    REFUTED IF it falls at any point. **That would be the best outcome available
    in this whole line**: it would mean the distractor failure is a
    small-evidence effect that experience repairs, and five axes of mechanism
    work would turn out to have been chasing something the data fixes by itself.

P5  **THE RAIL.** `surfaces` is 111 -- complete -- at every checkpoint, since
    `g39-01` reached 111 by 3,000.
    REFUTED IF it is not, which would mean the world is still arriving and the
    curve is confounded with coverage rather than being about evidence.

SCORECARD
---------
  P1  **HELD.** `link` is **0.9867** at both 12,000 and 24,000: it moves
            **0.0000** over the final doubling, against a 0.03 threshold. The
            curve is flat, and the plateau is at about 1,200 occasions per digit.

  P2  **HELD.** 0.9867 against the 0.9665 reported at 3,000. The extra pairings
            add evidence rather than confusion — the refutation clause worried
            that pairing each recording with more images would broaden the
            audio-to-image association and dilute it, and it does not.

  P3  **HELD, AND IT IS THE FINDING RATHER THAN A DETAIL.** `partition` improves
            **0.2549** (0.6667 to 0.9216) where `link` improves **0.0202**.

            **So the incumbent was far more penalised by the short stream**, and
            `g38-01`'s measured gap of 0.30 is largely a small-data effect. At
            convergence the walk leads **0.9867 to 0.9216** — a gap of 0.065,
            about a quarter of what was reported.

            The walk still wins, and it wins by much less than the record says.
            `g38-01` is corrected rather than softened.

            **`partition` is also NON-MONOTONE**: 0.6667, 0.5185, 0.8833, 0.9216.
            A hard partition is a discrete structure and a small change in scores
            flips whole components, so it does not improve smoothly. The walk's
            ranking has no such discontinuity and rises monotonically. That is an
            argument for the walk that no cell of `g38-01` could have shown.

  P4  **HELD, and it is the bad news.** `distractor` is **1.0000** at every
            checkpoint out to 24,000 occasions. The refutation clause called a
            fall *"the best outcome available in this whole line"* — it does not
            fall.

            **So experience does not repair it, and that is now settled rather
            than assumed.** Three scalar dials, the search budget, and stream
            length to eight passes. Five axes, none of them touches it.

  P5  **HELD.** `surfaces` is 111 at every checkpoint, so the world is complete
            throughout and the curve is about evidence rather than coverage. This
            is the rail and it fired correctly: `g39-01`'s early rows WERE
            confounded with an arriving world, and none of these are.


A CLEAN SCORECARD DESERVES SUSPICION, NOT SATISFACTION
--------------------------------------------------------
`CLAUDE.md` records this exact situation once before, at `g7-04`: all predictions
holding reads as a milestone and is mostly an artefact of the controls having
already run.

**That applies here.** These five predictions were written after `g39-01` had
shown the curve's shape, its endpoint, and that `distractor` was pinned at 1.0000
throughout. They were informed, not prescient. The one genuine uncertainty was
P3's magnitude, and the interesting part of the result — that the partition
closes three quarters of the gap — is something the prediction gestured at
without quantifying.


WHAT THIS CHANGES
-----------------
**`g39-01`'s alarm is bounded and mostly resolved.** Absolute figures at 3,000
occasions are lower bounds, but for the walk the shortfall is 0.02. The line's
headline numbers are close to their converged values.

**Except where they are not, and that is the incumbent.** Anything scored through
`equivalence_classes` at 3,000 occasions may be understating it by up to 0.25.
`g36-04`, `g36-05`, `g36-06` and `g35-02` all are.

**The distractor problem is now a structural fact rather than an open tuning
question.** Nothing in the mechanism as built refuses a surface present on every
occasion once the combiner is permissive enough to keep a hub edge. That is worth
stating plainly because five axes is enough evidence to stop looking for a sixth.


WHAT THIS DOES NOT SETTLE
-------------------------
**One arm.** `together` only. `alternating` is not extended and nothing here
transfers to it.

**The quantiser is still batch**, fitted on all 4,000 images before the stream
begins, so this remains prequential in the linking and batch in the perception.

**24,000 occasions is eight passes over 3,000 recordings.** The recordings
themselves never change, so this measures more PAIRINGS rather than more world.
Whether a genuinely larger world behaves the same is untested.
