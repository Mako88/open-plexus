WHAT IT SETTLES
----------------
**g17-01's conclusion survives its own correction.** "The model does not learn
word-level text at all" was measured on a mistrained readout, and with the target
fixed the same configuration learns **0.009 bits** over uniform -- if anything
less than the 0.038 it recorded.

So note 042's architecture pass, and the week of addressing work that followed
it, rest on a finding that is real. **What was void was my measurement of it, not
it.**


AND THE HALF THAT IS NOT A REPRODUCTION
----------------------------------------
    bias1   floor 9.932 against nostore 9.364     the store is worth -0.568

At g17-01's own configuration -- lr 0.05, no cap -- **the store is 0.568 bits
WORSE than not existing** once a prior is available. That is decision 139's claim
stated where g17-01 lives rather than at a tuned corner, and it is the strong
form: not "contributes nothing" but "actively costs", at the rate the project
used for every text sweep it ever ran.

Tuned (decision 139, lr 5e-6, cap 5.0) the same comparison is +0.002. So the rate
decides whether the store is harmless or harmful, and never whether it helps.
