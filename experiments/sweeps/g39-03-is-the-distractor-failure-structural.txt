Is the distractor failure structural, or was it measured too early?
====================================================================

Script: experiments/g39_03_is_the_distractor_failure_structural.py
Status: RUN, local, 2026-07-31, 8.4s. Predictions committed at `181c273`
        before the script was executed.

ANSWER: **STRUCTURAL. 0 of 24 settings meet the bar, and `distractor` is
        1.0000 in every single cell** — three exponents, four combiners, both
        stream lengths. The re-check at convergence did not rescue it.

        **And it identifies WHAT was doing the refusing all along.** `g36-06`
        measured the distractor falling to 0.0000 as the exponent rose — but
        that was under `equivalence_classes`. Under `reach` the exponent moves
        it **not at all**. So the thing excluding the distractor was never the
        statistic: it was the HARD CUT plus mutuality, which is exactly what a
        ranked walk removes.

        The trade is now nameable in one line: **a hard cut refuses the
        distractor and evicts the word; a ranked walk keeps the word and admits
        the distractor.** Five axes failed because none of them was the axis.

        Two predictions refuted, two held, and the rail held exactly.


IN PLAIN TERMS
--------------
There is one stubborn failure in this whole line of work. Something that is
present in every single moment -- a hum in the room, the word "the" -- gets
treated as though it were meaningful, because it turns up alongside everything.

Five different things have been tried against it and none worked. So the working
conclusion has been that it is a deep problem rather than a tuning problem.

**But there is a reason to doubt that conclusion, and it appeared only
yesterday.** All five of those attempts were measured after three thousand
moments -- and we have since found the machine was still learning fast at that
point. One of the two mechanisms being compared turned out to be much further
from its final state than the other. So an idea that looked refuted might simply
have been measured before its benefit arrived.

That is the most expensive mistake this project has a name for: throwing away a
good idea because the measurement was wrong.

So this re-runs the two most important of those attempts at the length where the
machine has actually stopped improving, and crosses them together, which nothing
has done. If nothing works there either, "it is a deep problem" stops being a
summary of five disappointments and becomes a claim with real evidence behind it.
If something does work, five conclusions need revisiting.

Either answer is worth having. The one that would be embarrassing is not asking.


WHAT IS BEING MEASURED
----------------------
    link         precision over the top-`k` image codes a word reaches
    covered      how many of the codes it SHOULD find it had a chance to score
    distractor   share of words admitting the ever-present distractor
    BOTH?        the whole question, as one column: link >= 0.80 AND
                 covered >= 0.80 AND distractor <= 0.05

**The coverage clause in `BOTH?` is not decoration.** `g38-03` reported a cell at
1.0000 precision over 0.0867 coverage as though it were the best in the table --
a mechanism that is right about a twentieth of the answer is not solving
anything. A criterion without that clause is the one `g36-06` wrote and then had
to retract, where a cell met the bar at a collapsed graph.


MEASURED ON
-----------
Local, one process. Scorer and stream construction match `g39-01` and `g39-02`.

    images       4,000   MNIST prefix
    recordings   3,000   FSDD, six speakers
    occasions   24,000   extended by RE-PAIRING, since an exact repeat is a
                         no-op for a ratio statistic
    codes           50   pinned, as everywhere in this line
    arm      together    where the failure is sharpest
    beam/depth   16, 1   g38-03's operating point
    seeds      0, 1, 2

Swept: `alpha` in {0.5, 0.75, 1.0} x `combiner` in {min, geometric, mean, max},
at 3,000 and 24,000 occasions. **The two axes have never been crossed.**

**Alpha 0 and 0.25 are excluded on evidence, not cost:** `g36-06` measured them
admitting the distractor at 1.0000 and 0.9333, which is the failing end of the
axis being probed.

SCORECARD
---------
  P1  **HELD, and it was written to fail.** `solved` is **0 of 24**. No exponent,
            no combiner, at neither stream length, keeps the link and refuses the
            distractor.

            Crossing the two axes was the last cheap thing available and it found
            nothing. **"Structural" now has a converged measurement behind it
            rather than being a summary of five disappointments.**

  P2  **HELD EXACTLY, and it is the rail.** `mean` at alpha 1.00 and 3,000
            occasions gives link **0.9665** at coverage **0.9933** — identical to
            `g38-03`'s reported cell to four decimals.

            So the stream construction has not drifted from the runs being
            re-checked, and every other row is comparable with them.

  P3  **REFUTED, AND THE REFUTATION IS THE MOST USEFUL LINE HERE.** The exponent
            was predicted to trade the distractor against the link. It moves the
            distractor **not at all** — 1.0000 at every alpha.

            `g36-06` measured the opposite: 1.0000, 0.9333, 0.1867, 0.0000,
            0.0000 as alpha rose. **The difference is the mechanism, not the
            data.** That run scored admission through `equivalence_classes`,
            which applies a derived cut and then requires mutuality. This one
            scores it through `reach`, which applies neither.

            **So the exponent never refused the distractor. The hard cut did.**
            An ever-present surface scores low under `conditional` and falls
            below the cliff; it also fails mutuality, since it is in everyone's
            list and nobody is in its. Remove the cut and the ranking still
            contains it — just further down, and `reach` reads the whole ranking.

            That reframes five axes at once: they were all statistics or budgets,
            and the mechanism doing the work was a structural constraint that the
            walk deliberately removed.

  P4  **REFUTED.** `min` was predicted to gain most from convergence, being
            furthest from its plateau. It gains **nothing**: link 0.9677 at both
            lengths and coverage 0.6000 to 0.5933, a slight fall.

            `geometric` gains most (0.9788 to 1.0000 at alpha 0.5), and `mean`
            gains its coverage (0.9933 to 1.0000). So `g39-02`'s
            *"furthest-from-plateau gains most"* does not generalise from the
            partition to the combiners.

  P5  **HELD.** `distractor` is 1.0000 at every setting at both lengths. Stream
            length changes it by exactly zero, which confirms `g39-02`'s finding
            was about the mechanism rather than about one setting.


AND A SECOND CORRECTION TO `g38-01`
------------------------------------
That run reported `min` reaching **nothing** — `reached` 0.00, link 0.0000 — and
this run has `min` at link **0.9677** over coverage **0.5933**.

**The difference is the beam.** `g38-01` ran beam 8; this runs beam 16. `g38-03`
measured coverage rising from 0.0867 at beam 2 to 0.9933 at beam 16, so a strict
combiner simply needs a wider window before image codes enter it at all.

So *"`min` reaches nothing"* is a beam-8 artefact and not a property of `min`.
The account attached to it — that `min` takes the weaker direction, which on a
hub edge is the small one — is still correct, and its consequence is milder than
reported: `min` costs **coverage**, not everything.

`g38-01`'s record is corrected in place. **That is the second correction to that
file today**, both from measuring at a setting it had pinned.


WHAT THIS CHANGES
-----------------
**Stop looking for a statistic that refuses the distractor.** Three exponents by
four combiners by two stream lengths says the ranking always contains it. The
refusal has to come from a structural constraint — a cut, mutuality, or something
not yet built — and not from how an edge is scored.

**And that is a genuine tension in John's proposal rather than a bug in it.** The
ranked walk exists to stop discarding edges, and discarding edges is what was
refusing the distractor. Those cannot both be had by tuning; something has to
supply the refusal that the cut used to.

**What the numbers say to do meanwhile.** Alpha 1.0 with `mean` or `max` gives
0.9867 link at 1.0000 coverage — the best cells in the table — so the operating
point is unchanged and the open problem is orthogonal to it.

**The alpha and combiner axes mostly move COVERAGE, not precision.** Link is
between 0.9664 and 1.0000 in all 24 cells while coverage ranges 0.5933 to 1.0000.
That was not visible in either earlier sweep and it means those axes are about
how much gets found, not how right it is.


WHAT THIS DOES NOT SETTLE
-------------------------
**One arm, one code count, one beam and depth.** `together`, 50 codes, beam 16,
depth 1.

**Alpha 0 and 0.25 were excluded on `g36-06`'s evidence** rather than re-measured
here, and that evidence was taken under the other mechanism. Given P3 found the
two mechanisms disagree about exactly this column, **that exclusion is now
weaker than it was when written** and should be re-checked before the low end is
called closed.

**Nothing here proposes the structural constraint it argues for.** Naming what a
mechanism needs is not building it, and `CLAUDE.md` is explicit that a diagnosis
is a claim about behaviour like any other.
