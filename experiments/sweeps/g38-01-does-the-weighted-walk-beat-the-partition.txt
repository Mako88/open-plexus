Does bounding the SEARCH beat bounding the REPRESENTATION?
===========================================================

Script: experiments/g38_01_does_the_weighted_walk_beat_the_partition.py
Status: RUN, local, 2026-07-31, 32.8s. Predictions committed at `da9d8c9`
        before the script was executed.

ANSWER: **Bounding the search beats bounding the representation, by the largest

IN PLAIN TERMS
--------------
Until now, each thing in this system kept only a handful of connections — the few
that stood out clearly — and everything else was ignored when questions were
asked. Three separate experiments this week failed for the same reason: something
important got pushed off that short list.

The worst case was clear. When a picture and a sound always arrived together,
they became each other's obvious partner and the **word** got pushed out. It
scored badly not because it was wrong but because it came seventh, and the
machine only ever looked at the first two.

So this tries the other arrangement. **Keep every connection, and limit how far a
question is allowed to travel instead.** Nothing is thrown away; a question just
has a budget. That is the owner of this project's suggestion, on the reasoning
that a concept genuinely IS a web of connections, and that if answering takes
longer, that is a problem to solve later rather than a reason not to try.

There is a specific reason it might not work, and it is written down so the run
can settle it rather than the argument.

A connection has two ends and they often disagree. The word *dog* is only
sometimes about any one picture, but a picture of a dog is almost always about
the word. So which end do you believe? Believe the weaker end and you are back to
pushing the word out. Believe the stronger end and you also start believing the
thing that is simply always present — the failure the whole design was corrected
for once already.

Those are opposite ends of one dial. The last time this project found a dial like
that, there was nothing useful in the middle. **This asks whether this one is
different**, and it watches both failures at once so a win on one cannot hide a
loss on the other.

One thing had to be replaced. The old arrangement could fail by lumping
everything into one blob, and we watched for that. The new one **cannot** blob —
which sounds better and is actually a hazard, because it means a careless score
would look good for reasons that have nothing to do with it working. So there is
a deliberately scrambled arm, and every number is read against that rather than
against zero.


WHAT IS BEING MEASURED
----------------------
    link@k       of the top-`k` IMAGE codes a word reaches, the share whose
                 majority digit is that word's. `k` is the number of image codes
                 that genuinely belong to that digit, so the bar is identical
                 for every arm and a longer list earns nothing
    link         the same over EVERYTHING reached, ignoring order. Reported
                 because ranking a set is a handicap the incumbent did not sign
                 up for, and `link@k` alone would understate it
    reached      how many image codes were available to score, per word. The
                 companion `link@k` needs: a perfect score over two codes and one
                 over twenty are different answers
    distractor   share of words admitting the ever-present distractor inside
                 their top `k`. `g32-01`'s falsifier, watched in the same run

**Chance for both link columns is about 0.10** and is printed by the run.

**`shuffled` is the floor and it is not zero.** A ranking cannot collapse, so the
alarm the partition had — mean class size — does not exist here. Reading any
number against zero would be `g32-01`'s mistake repeated on a new mechanism.


MEASURED ON
-----------
Local, one process. Streams IMPORTED from `g36_04` rather than restated.

    images       4,000   MNIST prefix
    recordings   3,000   FSDD, six speakers
    occasions    3,000
    codes           50   **PINNED from g36-04**, where it was the middle of

    noise            2   other words per occasion

    depth            2   so a word may reach an image code directly, or through

    seeds      0, 1, 2

Arms: `together` and `alternating`, crossed with six methods — `classes` (the
incumbent), the four combiners, and `shuffled`.

**Why `together` is the cell that matters.** It is where the bound demonstrably
failed: the word survives it for **0.0200** of image codes and link purity is
**0.6667** against 0.8476 in `alternating`. If the walk cannot beat the partition
there, it will not beat it anywhere.


PREDICTIONS
-----------
P1  **THE HEADLINE.** On `together`, at least one combiner beats `classes` on
    `link@k` by more than 0.05.
    REFUTED IF none does. Then bounding the search buys nothing over bounding the
    representation on the exact cell chosen because the representation bound
    failed, and the ceiling should stay — which would be a clean negative on
    John's proposal and would save building the rest of it.

P2  **`min` DOES NOT HELP, and this is the doubt registered at `strength`'s own
    definition before any of this ran.** On `together`, `min` scores within 0.05
    of `classes` on `link@k`, because it takes the weaker direction and on a hub
    edge that is the small one — reproducing the eviction rather than repairing
    it.
    REFUTED IF `min` clearly beats `classes`. Then the eviction was about the
    hard CUT rather than about which direction is believed, and the combiner axis
    matters less than expected.

P3  `max` READMITS THE DISTRACTOR. Its `distractor` column is above 0.5 in at
    least one arm, because an ever-present thing has a backward direction of 1.0
    and `max` takes it.
    REFUTED IF `max` keeps the distractor out. **That is the best available
    outcome**: it would mean the axis HAS an interior — unlike `damped`'s — and
    the lopsided hub edge can be kept without paying `g32-01`'s price.

P4  NO REGRESSION ON THE GOOD CELL. On `alternating`, where `classes` already
    scores well, the best combiner is within 0.05 of it.
    REFUTED IF every combiner is clearly worse there. Then the walk trades a good
    case for a bad one and is not a replacement, only an alternative for hubs.

P5  THE FLOOR IS REAL AND NOT ZERO. `shuffled` scores near chance 0.10 on
    `link@k`, not 0.00.
    REFUTED IF it scores near zero, which would mean the metric has no positive
    floor and the other rows can be read directly. **This is the rail**: if
    `shuffled` scores well, the metric is broken and nothing else here is
    readable.


COST
----
Estimated from `g36-06`'s measured 25.5s for 10 cells at this code count. This is
12 cells, but each `reach` expands up to `beam**depth` = 64 nodes per word and
scores every partner of each — heavier per cell than a single walk. `classes`
and `shuffled` are cheap. **60 to 240 seconds**, local.

Recorded after the run: **32.8s**, against an estimate of 60 to 240 seconds.
Below the range this time — the beam expands far fewer nodes than `beam**depth`
in practice, because most surfaces have few partners with a non-zero score.

SCORECARD
---------
  P1  **HELD, and it is the largest improvement measured on this line.** On
            `together`, `mean` scores **0.9589** on `link@k` against `classes`'s
            **0.6667** — a gain of 0.29, against a predicted bar of 0.05.

            **The word was never missing, only out of reach.** `g36-05` put it at
            rank 6.70 under a bound that keeps two partners. A search allowed to
            look eight deep finds it, and the stored table did not change by one
            count.

  P2  **REFUTED AS WRITTEN, AND THE DOUBT IT ENCODED WAS RIGHT.** The prediction
            was that `min` would land within 0.05 of `classes`. It lands
            **0.6667 below it**, reaching nothing at all.

            The arithmetic is the entry. For a word `w` and image code `c`,
            `min` takes `conditional(c, w)` — small, because `w` is common — at
            roughly 0.07. For the distractor `d`, it takes `conditional(w, d)` at
            roughly 0.28, because `d`'s own count is spread across every
            occasion rather than concentrated. **So the distractor's weaker
            direction is still stronger than a true partner's**, and the top of
            every word's list is distractor and noise words.

            The registered refutation clause — *"REFUTED IF `min` clearly beats
            `classes`"* — did not fire. So the direction was right and the
            magnitude was wrong by an order of magnitude, and the honest reading
            is that the doubt recorded at `strength`'s definition was correct
            while the number attached to it was a guess.

  P3  **REFUTED, and it looked like good news until it was checked.** `max` was
            predicted to readmit the distractor above 0.5. Its `distractor`
            column is **0.0000** in both arms.

            **That is an artefact of tie-breaking and not a property.** `max`
            takes the stronger direction, and for an image code that is
            `conditional(w, c)` at near 1.0 — while the distractor's backward
            direction is exactly 1.0. Everything saturates at the top, ties are
            broken by ascending surface id, and image codes hold ids 0-49 while
            the distractor holds 110. **It is excluded by its number, not by its
            score.**

            The tell is `link@k` = **0.1000**, exactly chance and exactly
            `shuffled`. A clean distractor column beside a chance-level link is a
            mechanism that has stopped discriminating, not one that has learned
            to refuse.

            So the axis does NOT have a usable interior in the way P3's
            refutation clause hoped: `max` does not keep the lopsided hub edge
            while paying nothing, it destroys the ranking.

  P4  **HELD, and better than held.** On `alternating`, where `classes` already
            scores 0.9220, `mean` gives **0.9829**. The prediction was that the
            best combiner would be within 0.05 rather than clearly worse; it is
            0.06 BETTER.

            So the walk is not a hub-only alternative that trades a good case for
            a bad one. It wins in both.

  P5  **HELD, and it is the rail that makes the rest readable.** `shuffled`
            scores **0.1000** and **0.1067** on `link@k`, which is chance and not
            zero. Every number above is read against that.

            It also caught something: `shuffled`'s `distractor` is **0.0333**,
            not 0. A random ranking admits the distractor about a thirtieth of
            the time by luck, so 0.0000 is genuinely below chance and 1.0000 is
            genuinely total.


WHAT THIS CHANGES
-----------------
**John's proposal is right about the mechanism and the numbers are on its side.**
Keeping every edge and bounding the search instead recovers a result the bounded
representation could not reach, without changing one stored count. `link@k` goes
0.6667 to 0.9589 where the bound had failed, and 0.9220 to 0.9829 where it had
not.

**And it is not adoptable as it stands.** `mean` fails `g32-01`'s falsifier
completely — every word admits the ever-present distractor. That refutation cost
this project its original design once and the correction is the reason
`conditional` is the deployed statistic. Trading it back for a better link would
be undoing a measured result to buy an unmeasured one.

**THE THIRD DIAL OF THIS SHAPE, and that is now a pattern rather than an
incident.** `damped`'s exponent had the word at one end and the distractor at the
other with nothing in between (`g36-06`). The combiner has the same shape:
`min` protects nothing, `max` discriminates nothing, `mean` wins the link and
loses the distractor. **Three scalar knobs, three times the same two-ended
failure.** That is evidence the conflict is structural rather than untuned, and
the next proposal should not be a fourth knob.

**What the arithmetic points at instead.** The distractor's damage enters through
its BACKWARD direction being exactly 1.0 — it is present whenever anything is.
That is not a property a combiner can weigh away, because it is true. It is a
property of the surface, and a rule that looked at a surface's own breadth —
*how many different things is this present with* — would refuse it without
consulting any edge. Untried, not a knob, and the second proposal in this line
the measurements point at rather than merely permit.


WHAT THIS DOES NOT SETTLE
-------------------------
**One code count, pinned at 50 from `g36-04`**, named as this run's standing risk
before it ran. The beam and depth interact with how many codes a digit has, and
that is not swept.

**`beam` and `depth` were not swept either.** 8 and 2 were chosen so a word could
reach an image code directly or through an audio code. Whether the win grows or
shrinks with a larger budget is exactly John's *"how deep may a query walk"*
question and is unmeasured.

**Nothing here is distributed.** `reach` costs one remote read per candidate, the
same as `conditional`, but the TRAVERSAL cost is new and `g33-03`'s 439 messages
per walk was measured on the bounded version. The unbounded figure is unknown.

**`geometric` is unexplained.** It scores 0.6667 on `together` and 0.1737 on
`alternating` — better than `min`, far worse than `mean`, and inconsistent
between arms. No account is offered because none was checked.
