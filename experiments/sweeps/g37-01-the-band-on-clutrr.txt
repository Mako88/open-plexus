How wide is CLUTRR-symbolic's measurement band?
================================================

Script: experiments/g37_01_the_band_on_clutrr.py
Status: RUN, local, 2026-07-31, 1.7s. **The floor table was computed in scratch
        before this artifact existed**, so the prediction below is WEAK and
        marked -- see the section on it.

ANSWER: **Wide enough, and kill-list item #1 is not blocked for the reason
        recorded.** The honest floor on the test split is **0.1370** overall and
        0.16 to 0.27 in the usable hop buckets, against published graph-only
        references from 0.39 (the weakest, GCN at 10 hops) to about 0.97 (R5).
        So the band is between **0.20 and 0.79**, against `closure`'s **0.092**.
        Even the most pessimistic reading is more than twice as wide.


IN PLAIN TERMS
--------------
The project has a list of things that could kill it. Item one is *"does training
on relationships actually buy reasoning?"*, and it has been sitting marked
**blocked** — not because the idea failed, but because there was supposedly no
test sensitive enough to tell a good answer from a bad one.

A test is only useful if there is room between *"a machine that learned nothing"*
and *"the best anyone has managed"*. If that gap is narrow, every result lands
inside the noise and nothing is learned from running it. The gap on the test we
built ourselves is small — about nine points.

This checks a test we did **not** build: CLUTRR, a published benchmark about
family relationships, whose data has been sitting fetched in this repository the
whole time. Work out who someone is to someone else, given a chain of other
relationships, and never being told the answer directly.

**The gap there is between twenty and seventy-nine points.** So the instrument
exists, other people have already published scores on the exact version we have,
and the thing that was blocking item one was a misreading of our own notes rather
than a missing test.

That last part is the uncomfortable half and it is written down deliberately.


WHAT WAS ACTUALLY BLOCKING IT, AND IT WAS OURS
------------------------------------------------
`docs/options/clutrr-symbolic.md` said, correctly:

    Results are "CLUTRR-symbolic" and published text numbers are not comparable.

**That sentence is true.** The NLU baselines in the literature read prose, and
this project does not generate prose, so their numbers say nothing about it.

**It was read as a different sentence** — *no published CLUTRR number is
comparable* — and that reading is false. The standard evaluation in the
literature is the **noiseless graph-based** version, trained on k=2,3 and tested
on k in [2,10]. That is `gen_train23_test2to10`, which is the split already on
disk here.

This is `CLAUDE.md` rule 1's borrowed-claim failure with the borrowing happening
**inside this repository**: a claim filed under *established*, sitting upstream of
a decision, that no downstream measurement could ever reach — because everything
downstream was conditioned on it.

The tell was grammatical and available at any time: the record says *text*
numbers, and the conclusion drawn was about *published* numbers.


WHAT IS BEING MEASURED
----------------------
    majority       the share the commonest answer takes. **The floor.** A
                   constant predictor is free, so this is what any mechanism
                   must be read against
    rep<=2         share of the bucket whose most-repeated entity appears in at
                   most two edges -- note 059's axis
    maj|rep<=2     the floor ON that arm, reported separately because it MOVES
    n|rep<=2       how many puzzles that leaves, because a floor over 32
                   puzzles is not a floor

**Chance is 0.0500 and is NOT the floor.** Reporting it as one would overstate
every result by about nine points at 10 hops. `g32-01` cost this project a
published figure by reading a metric against zero when its floor was 0.5.


MEASURED ON
-----------
Local, one process, no model, no seed, no training. A property of the data.

    data        CLUTRR gen_train23_test2to10, fetched, gitignored
    layout      kinship -- chosen on measurement, collisions 35.9% -> 7.7%
    relations   20 in the loader's fixed vocabulary; 18 used as answers
    splits      train 9,074, validation 2,020, test 1,146

**No seeds, and that is correct rather than a shortcut.** A constant predictor's
score on a fixed file has no variance. Rule 3's seed-count calibration is about
stochastic mechanisms; there is nothing stochastic here.

into an artifact is worth doing regardless — `g36-01`'s headline was a lucky draw
that survived in the record precisely because its diagnostic lived in scratch.

P2 was NOT known and is a real commitment.

SCORECARD
---------
  P1  HELD. The usable buckets floor at 0.1609 to 0.2667. Against the weakest
            published graph reference the band is about **0.20**; against the
            strongest, about **0.79**. `closure`'s is **0.092**.

  P2  **HELD, and it is the finding that changes how this instrument must be
            read.** `maj|rep<=2` is higher than the unconditional majority
            everywhere past 4 hops, and by a lot:

                 6 hops   0.3810  against  0.2336
                 7 hops   0.2951  against  0.1806
                 9 hops   0.3111  against  0.1681

            **The clean arm is easier by base rate**, because removing repeated
            entities removes the harder puzzles rather than an unrelated
            nuisance. So the primary arm's floor is a different number at every
            hop, and a `rep<=2` column read across hops is several scales printed
            as one -- `g35-02`'s confound, waiting on a new instrument.

            The practical consequence: **report the floor beside every cell**, or
            report a floor-relative quantity. Not the raw accuracy.


THE BORROWED REFERENCE NUMBERS, AND EXACTLY HOW FAR TO TRUST THEM
------------------------------------------------------------------
Recorded here so anything quoting them can point at one place, and so the
verification status travels with them rather than being re-established.

Trained on k=2,3, tested on longer chains, noiseless graph-based CLUTRR --
the same configuration as `gen_train23_test2to10`:

    R5             0.98   0.99   0.98   0.96   0.97   0.98   0.97
    CTP_A          0.99   0.99   0.99   0.96   0.94   0.89   0.90
    GNTP           0.49   0.45   0.38   0.37   0.32   0.31   0.31
    GAT_s          0.91   0.76   0.54   0.56   0.54   0.55   0.45
    GCN_s          0.84   0.68   0.53   0.47   0.42   0.45   0.39

    our majority   0.1895 0.1609 0.2336 0.1806 0.2667 0.1681 0.1849

**PROVENANCE, and it is not clean.** Read from an ar5iv HTML rendering of R5
(arXiv 2205.06454) Table 2, via a summarising fetch. **The PDF was not read
directly** — extraction failed on every PDF attempted, which is the honest reason
rather than a choice. A second source (arXiv 2407.17396) was consulted and agrees
on model ordering and magnitude in the easier k=2,3,4 training setting, where it
reports GAT at 0.99 falling to 0.68 and GCN at 0.94 falling to 0.41.

`CLAUDE.md` rule 1: this is a SUMMARISED claim and must not gate a design choice
on its own. **What makes it usable is that the conclusion does not depend on it
being right.** The band is wide whether the reference is 0.39 or 0.98, and the
two sources' disagreement is smaller than the distance to the floor.

**What would settle it properly**, in order of cost: read one PDF locally, or run
a strong reference here. The second is better and is the obvious next step, since
`GOALS.md` G0 asks for a reference *measured*, not cited.


THE THREE CAVEATS, AND THEY BIND
---------------------------------
**The 2-hop test bucket is 38 puzzles with a floor of 0.5000.** Nothing from it
is quotable. 3 hops is 105 at 0.4286 and is nearly as bad. **The usable range
starts at 4 hops**, which is also where the published references start reporting.

**The clean arm shrinks to 32 puzzles at 10 hops.** Depth and entity repetition
are confounded in the data itself and no choice of arm removes both. A result at
10 hops on the clean arm has a standard error around 0.08 from sample size alone.

**Train has ZERO repeated-entity puzzles and test has up to 73%.** That is note
059's finding and it is a property of CLUTRR, not of anything here. It means the
generalisation being tested is partly *to repeated entities*, which is this
project's measured weak point (decision 103: 0.884 -> 0.303).


WHAT THIS DOES NOT SETTLE
-------------------------
**The reference numbers are BORROWED and only partly verified.** They were read
from ar5iv HTML renderings of two papers via a summarising fetch, not from the
PDFs. Two independent sources agree on ordering and magnitude. `CLAUDE.md` rule 1
says a summarised claim must be marked and must not gate a design choice on its
own -- **the conclusion here survives the full disagreement between the two
sources**, which is the only reason it is allowed to carry one.

**No frozen-substrate control has been run.** G0 wants a random frozen substrate
measured, and that is a model this script does not build. The floor is the
constant predictor, which is the stricter of the two.

**Nothing says our mechanism can score anything here.** A wide band means the
instrument can SEE a difference. It says nothing about which direction one will
be found in, and `g14-01` is the reminder: on `closure` the local rule came in
**below** the base rate.
