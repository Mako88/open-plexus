SCORING
-------
  P1  CONFIRMED, and it is the headline. `walk` beats `concat` by **+0.269**.
      **Decision 107's verdict does not survive the primitives moving.** It
      declined the traversal because "a perfect traversal buys 0.05", computed
      when steps 1 and 3 were 0.710 and 0.677. They are 1.000 at out-degree 1
      now, and the traversal is worth five times what it was costed at.

  P2  CONFIRMED BY SIGN AND REFUTED BY MARGIN: +0.008 +/-0.018, comfortably
      inside 2 SE. **Search does not beat traversal overall.** Recorded as the
      tie it is -- the prediction asked whether search beats walk and the honest
      answer is "not distinguishably".

  P3  CONFIRMED. 0.604 against a first-relation floor of 0.466. **No mechanism
      on this task had ever cleared that floor** -- `concat` sat at 0.327, below
      even a model that just guesses from the queried subject's own relation.

  P4  REFUTED. `search8` is 0.024 WORSE than `search4`, not within 0.02 of it,
      and the margin is 6 SE. More branches actively hurt: every extra candidate
      is another chance for a wrong branch whose endpoint happens to score well.
      The verifier is good, not perfect, and widening the beam samples its
      errors more often.

  P5  CONFIRMED. Best arm 0.604 against g13-02's 1.000 ceiling. The ceiling
      holds steps 1 and 3 at their out-degree-1 value, and the gap is how often
      the walk fails to reach that regime.


THE OUT-DEGREE SPLIT IS THE FINDING
------------------------------------
    search4 - walk at out-degree 1       -0.054
    search4 - walk at out-degree >= 2    +0.092

**Search does precisely what it was built to do, and damages the case it was not
built for.** At out-degree 1 there is exactly one relation and nothing to choose
between; searching there can only replace a correct greedy pick with a branch
whose endpoint scored higher by luck. At out-degree >= 2 it is worth +0.092,
which is the largest single gain on the ambiguous case anything has produced.

The two nearly cancel because the test set is about half of each. **That is why
the overall number is a tie, and it means the tie is not evidence against
search** -- it is evidence that search should not run unconditionally.

**The obvious next mechanism is a gate**: search only where the first decode is
ambiguous. Whether ambiguity is detectable from the decode alone -- as opposed to
from the out-degree, which is task structure a running system cannot read -- is
the open question, and decision 93 is the warning attached to it: every
identity-free confidence signal it measured reached 0.628 against 0.500 for
guessing. The relevant quantity here is different (the gap between the top two
candidates of one decode, not the confidence of a retrieval), but the precedent
says measure it before building on it.


WHAT THIS DOES NOT SAY
-----------------------
That search is worthless. It is the only arm that moves the ambiguous case, and
ambiguity is what decision 108 named as the blocker. What it says is that
searching everywhere is not how to spend it.

Nor does it license quoting 0.604 as the ceiling of the approach. Every arm here
runs the walk unconditionally at a fixed depth of 2, on one task, at one width.
