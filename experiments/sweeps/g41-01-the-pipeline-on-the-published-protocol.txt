Does 0.8578 survive being reported properly?
============================================

Script: experiments/g41_01_the_pipeline_on_the_published_protocol.py
Status: PREDICTIONS REGISTERED, run pending.

**A TIMING PROBE RAN FIRST AND ITS NUMBERS ARE VISIBLE TO ME.** One seed, width
64, both beam widths, about 12 seconds. It was run to cost the sweep and it
smoke-tested the script, and then it showed the whole result at that cell.

`CLAUDE.md` is explicit that a prediction written after a control has run is a
weaker test than it looks, and the honest response is to say which came first
rather than to write four confident predictions and collect a clean scorecard.
So every prediction below is labelled **SEEN** or **GENUINE**, and only the
GENUINE ones are evidence that anything was understood in advance.


IN PLAIN TERMS
--------------
We have a number for this system on somebody else's reasoning benchmark: 0.8578.
It is a good number — the published systems on the same benchmark run from about
0.39 to 0.97.

But it is a single average over 1,146 puzzles of wildly different difficulty, and
this project has already written down, twice, why that is not allowed here. The
easy puzzles and the hard puzzles are mixed together. It was measured once, at
settings copied from an older experiment that nobody has since varied. And it was
never compared against the score you would get by ignoring the puzzle entirely and
always giving the same answer.

This run changes nothing about how the system works. It reports the same
machinery honestly: broken out by how hard each puzzle is, with the do-nothing
score printed beside it, at eight different random starts instead of one, and
with the two copied settings actually varied.

A good number that survives this is worth quoting. One that does not was never
real, and it is much cheaper to find that out now than after it has been built on.


WHAT IS BEING MEASURED
----------------------
    accuracy    per hop bucket, 2 to 10, NEVER pooled. Hops 2-3 are the only
                depths present in TRAIN, so they are recall and 4-10 are
                generalisation
    recovery    whether the walk commits to the true chain of relations. The
                diagnostic that separates "the walk lost the chain" from "the
                fold named it wrong"
    subsets     `all`, and `clean` = note 059's max_appearances <= 2. Reported
                BESIDE each other, never instead, because the floor differs
                between them and g35-02's confound is a column of several scales
                printed as one

The arms, all scored on the same puzzles:

    majority    the ACHIEVABLE floor -- always answer the commonest TRAIN
                relation. NOT the test bucket's own majority, which g37-03
                established is an ORACLE floor no model can reach
    gap         fold with no fill for a missing rule
    random      fill a gap with a random relation. Note 088's bar
    wrong-delta THE CONTROL. A deliberately wrong displacement. It must land at
                or below `random`, and if it does not, "filling helps" is the
                finding rather than "this displacement helps"
    delta       the mechanism
    contrastive the learned-vector fill from g23-01/02

Swept: `width` over {32, 64, 128} and `beam width` over {4, 8}, at 8 seeds.
Both are constants carried from note 065's configuration and neither has been
varied since. `CLAUDE.md` names sweeping one arm and not the other by name, and
g37-03 swept the reference while leaving exactly these untouched.

**Eight seeds rather than three, on purpose.** The probe returned 1.0000 in
several cells. A failure occurring in one draw of eight is missed by three seeds
about 67% of the time, so a clean sweep of three is the EXPECTED outcome even
when a one-in-eight failure exists (`CLAUDE.md`, the g36-01 calibration). The
report prints the WORST seed beside the mean for the `delta` arm and for
recovery.


WHAT IS NOT CHANGED, AND WHY IT IS NOT
--------------------------------------
**Both aids stay in.** The walk is handed `len(chain)`, so it is told its own
depth; and *"deltas add"* is arithmetic supplied by hand rather than discovered
(note 090 says so in its own text).

Keeping them is what makes this comparable to note 091's 0.8578. Removing them is
a separate run, because a fall would otherwise be unattributable between "the
protocol was generous" and "the aid was doing the work" — which is `g37-02`'s
mistake one level up.


MEASURED ON
-----------
Local, one process.

    data        CLUTRR gen_train23_test2to10, TEST split, 1,146 puzzles
    rules       97, learned from TRAIN (note 066's bootstrap)
    deltas      20, from TRAIN loop constraints, null space dimension 1
    search      beam, branches 4 fixed, width swept
    layout      kinship

The data does not move with the seed. CLUTRR is a fixed file, so a seed varies
model initialisation, the contrastive vectors and the fill draws only. Variance
here is a statement about the model, not about the task.


PREDICTIONS
-----------
P1  **GENUINE.** WIDTH DOES NOT MATTER AND THE CARRIED 64 IS NOT THE ISSUE. The
    `delta` arm at 10 hops moves by less than 0.05 across {32, 64, 128} at fixed
    beam.
    REFUTED IF the spread exceeds 0.05. Then a second carried constant was
    load-bearing and every earlier CLUTRR figure is at an untuned width.

P2  **GENUINE, and it is the one this run exists for.** THE CELLS AT 1.0000 DO
    NOT ALL SURVIVE EIGHT SEEDS. At least one cell reporting 1.0000 at seed 0
    has a worst seed below 1.0000.
    REFUTED IF every 1.0000 cell is 1.0000 on all eight. Then the perfection is
    real and may be written as "every" — which is the word the g36-01
    calibration exists to stop being written on three seeds.

P3  **SEEN, so this is a summary and not a commitment.** BEAM WIDTH 4 IS
    UNDERTUNED AT DEPTH. The probe gave `delta` at 10 hops 0.7059 at beam 4
    against 0.7815 at beam 8, subset `all`. This says only whether that holds at
    8 seeds and 3 widths.

P4  **SEEN in part.** THE WRONG-DELTA CONTROL DOES NOT FIRE AT 2 HOPS. Every arm
    scored 1.0000 there in the probe, so the bucket cannot separate the
    mechanism from filling anything at all. GENUINE half: it fires at every
    bucket from 4 hops up, at every width and beam, on all 8 seeds — meaning
    `delta` exceeds `wrong-delta` in all of them.
    REFUTED IF any bucket from 4 up has `wrong-delta` at or above `delta`. Then
    the mechanism is not separated from filling at that depth.

P5  **GENUINE.** THE POOLED 0.8578 IS NOT REPRESENTATIVE OF THE DEEP BUCKETS.
    The `delta` arm at 10 hops, subset `all`, is below 0.8578 at every width and
    beam.
    REFUTED IF any cell at 10 hops meets or beats the pooled figure. Then
    pooling did not flatter the result and the protocol complaint costs nothing.
