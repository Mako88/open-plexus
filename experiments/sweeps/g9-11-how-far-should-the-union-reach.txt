How far should the union reach?
===============================

Script: experiments/g9_05_the_tag.py, with --window swept
Status: RUN. 15 of 15 cells, no refusals. Both wiring checks passed. The
        capacity was MISTUNED by my own error -- see THE DESIGN ERROR.
Run:    https://github.com/Mako88/open-plexus/actions/runs/30248615080

ANSWER: the tag's marks are worth +1.2 when the window is unmatched and NOTHING
        when it is matched. The combined gate is not two signals combining --
        it is insurance against a reach you cannot set correctly.


THE RESULT
-----------
`combined` minus `reward` in the SAME cell. Both arms use the same reach and the
same capacity, so the difference is exactly what the tag's marks add.

    reach    delay 1   delay 8   delay 20
        1      -0.01     +1.19      +0.09
        2      -0.01     +1.21      +0.11
        4      -0.01     +1.23      +0.10
        8      +0.01     -0.01      +0.11
       16      -0.01     +0.00      +0.13

And the arms themselves at delay 8:

    reach        reward   combined       tag
        1         -1.53      -0.34     -0.35
        2         -1.53      -0.32     -0.35
        4         -1.54      -0.31     -0.35
        8         +0.23      +0.22     -0.35
       16           --       +0.23     -0.35

**At reach 8 the window is matched to delay 8 and the tag adds nothing** --
-0.01, which is the degeneracy every earlier control found. **At reaches 1, 2 and
4 the window is unmatched and catastrophic at -1.53, and the tag drags it back to
-0.31.** That is the entire value of the union, and it appears only where the
window is wrong.

So the combined gate is not "one signal says it is a binding, the other says it
is the rewarded one". It is **a floor under a badly set reach**. Which is the
useful reading for a deployed system, because a node cannot know the delay and
therefore cannot set the reach -- the tag's contribution is precisely that it
covers the case where the guess was wrong.


PREDICTIONS, SCORED
--------------------
1. **CONFIRMED, far beyond the guess.** Predicted the union's advantage survives
   at reach 1 and 2 at "+0.20 or better". It is +1.19 and +1.21. The reasoning
   was right and the magnitude was not imagined: the advantage is not an
   improvement on a good result, it is the rescue of a catastrophic one.

2. **CONFIRMED exactly.** Predicted combined at delay 20 lands within 0.05 of the
   tag alone at reach 1. Both are -1.51. Nothing here reaches delay 20, so the
   window contributes nothing and the union IS the tag.

3. **CONFIRMED, and it is a sharp cliff.** The window's collapse sits exactly at
   the reach that matches the delay: -1.53 at reaches 1, 2 and 4, +0.23 at reach
   8. That reproduces g9-03 on a new axis, at a different width, months later.

4. **REFUTED.** Predicted the best combined reach is interior. `tools/grid.py`:
   *all 3 arms chose an edge of the grid [1, 2, 4, 8, 16] (2 at the top, 16)*.
   The delay-20 row is uniformly catastrophic so its "best" is arbitrary, but the
   check fired honestly and the numbers above 8 are bounds.

5. **CONFIRMED.** The `tag` row is flat across reach to **0.000** spread -- it
   never reads the dial. The arms are independent and the run counts.


THE DESIGN ERROR, WHICH IS MINE AND IS THE SAME ONE I FLAGGED
--------------------------------------------------------------
`slots` was fixed at 4 because that was g9-10's best for the combined arm. **It
was g9-10's best AT NODE 32.** This grid runs at width 64 with one partition, so
the node is 64, and g9-10's own numbers show `slots` 4 getting worse as the node
grows: +0.06 at node 8, +0.06 at 16, -0.02 at 32. At node 64 the tag alone scores
-0.35, which is exactly that trend continuing.

So every absolute number here is at a badly mistuned capacity, and the tag's
arm in particular is nowhere near its best.

**This is the error I named as g9-09's standing risk and then repeated** -- a
working point carried from one width to another. Naming a risk in a sweep file
does not stop it recurring in the next one.

**What survives it.** The reported quantity is `combined - reward` within a cell,
and both arms share the capacity. The difference isolates the tag's contribution
whatever the capacity is, which is why the summariser prints it. So the finding
-- marks are worth +1.2 against an unmatched window and nothing against a matched
one -- stands. **The absolute recoveries do not**, and no number in the arm
tables should be quoted as the tag's or the union's performance.

A re-run at `slots` 16, which g9-10 puts near the optimum at delay 8, would give
absolute numbers worth quoting. It is cheap and it is in BACKLOG.


IN PLAIN TERMS
---------------
Two filters were combined and the pair beat either alone. One of them has a
setting nobody has ever changed: how far back it looks. It has been fixed at
"the last nine things" for every measurement ever taken here.

Nine is generous. Each of those nine is kept whether it looks useful or not, so
eight of them are usually junk that crowds the memory. If looking back at only
the last one or two works nearly as well, the pair gets most of its benefit at a
fraction of the cost — and the setting that has been silently carried through
ten sweeps was simply too big.

If instead the benefit disappears when the reach shrinks, then the pair is
working for a reason nobody has yet named, and that is worth knowing before
anything else is built on it.


WHY THIS RUN EXISTS
--------------------
[g9-10](g9-10-does-the-best-capacity-track-the-node.txt) found the combined gate
scoring **+0.26 at `slots` 4** where the tag alone scores -0.02 -- the highest
tag-family recovery anywhere in this project. `REWARD_WINDOW` is frozen at 8 in
that cell and in every other combined cell ever run.

**A counting control says the union is dominated by that reach.** Recall x
precision, 8 sequences, no training:

    delay 8    window alone 0.112   combined 1/2/4/8: 0.109 0.108 0.106 0.096
    delay 20   window alone 0.000   tag 8 alone 0.008   combined 8: 0.003

At delay 8 the union's recall is pinned at 100% BY THE WINDOW -- a union cannot
subtract -- and its precision approaches window-alone from below, so the tag's
marks only dilute. At delay 20 the union is WORSE than the tag alone, because it
adds nine writes per capture and no recall.

**And that control cannot settle this sweep**, which is why the sweep exists. It
ranks window-alone ABOVE combined at delay 8 (0.112 against 0.106) where g9-10
measured the reverse (+0.23 against +0.26). So whatever the union adds is in
WHICH writes it keeps rather than how many, and a count of what is kept is blind
to it. That is a stated limit of a tool used three times tonight, and the first
case where it failed.


THE GRID
---------
  reach       1, 2, 4, 8, 16   the window's span, on BOTH the window arm and the
                               combined one. Five values so grid.py can find an
                               interior; 8 reproduces every earlier cell and is
                               the wiring check
  delay       1, 8, 20         1 is where note 027's leak makes the task trivial
                               and is included ONLY as a control on that
  slots       4, FIXED         g9-10's best for the combined arm
  fade        0.95, FIXED
  width       64, partitions 1

  seeds       1, 2, 3
  lr          0.05, FIXED on every arm

15 jobs. `tag` does not use the reach at all, so its row is a second wiring
check: it must be flat across the whole grid, and if it is not, the arms are not
independent.


PREDICTIONS
------------
1. **The combined gate's advantage over the window SURVIVES at reach 1 and 2.**
   Guess: +0.20 or better at delay 8, against the window's own score at the same
   reach. The reasoning is that the union's gain is in which writes it adds --
   the tag contributes binding-like writes the window missed -- and that
   contribution does not depend on how far the window reaches.

2. **The combined gate at delay 20 improves as the reach SHRINKS.** At reach 8
   it is worse than the tag alone, because nine useless writes per capture cost
   more than the window contributes when it cannot reach the binding. At reach 1
   it adds two, so it should approach the tag alone from below rather than
   sitting well beneath it. Guess: within 0.05 of the tag at reach 1.

3. **The window arm alone has its cliff at the reach that matches the delay**,
   reproducing g9-03 on a new axis and at a different width. Positive at delays
   1 and 8 for reach >= 8, negative at delay 20 for every reach here. This is a
   wiring check against a sweep run months ago.

4. **The best combined reach is INTERIOR**, between 1 and 8 rather than at
   either end. Too small and the window contributes nothing; too large and it
   contributes junk.

5. **The `tag` row is flat across reach**, because the tag arm never reads it.
   If it moves, the arms share state and nothing in the run counts.

Most likely wrong: **1.** It assumes the union's benefit is additive in the
tag's contribution, and the counting control already shows the union behaves
non-monotonically across intervals -- protecting more changes the store, which
changes what the tag marks next. That feedback has already produced a union that
captured LESS than one of its own arms, so "the tag's contribution does not
depend on the reach" may simply be false.


WHAT WOULD REFUTE WHAT
-----------------------
- advantage survives at reach 1     -> the union is cheap, and every combined
                                       cell measured so far paid eight writes
                                       per capture for nothing
- advantage needs reach 8           -> the union is not adding binding-like
                                       writes, it is adding SPAN, and the
                                       combined gate is a window with a longer
                                       arm rather than two signals
- combined never beats the window   -> g9-10's +0.26 was noise or a width
  at any reach                         artefact, and the cheapest explanation of
                                       the whole combined result is wrong
- the tag row moves with reach      -> the arms are not independent. Nothing in
                                       the run counts; fix the wiring first
