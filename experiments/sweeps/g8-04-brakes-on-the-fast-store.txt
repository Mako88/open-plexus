Does bounding the fast store make skew measurable, and does it flatter anything?
================================================================================

Script: experiments/g8_04_capped.py
Status: **ANSWERED BY ITS OWN CONTROL, AND NOT DISPATCHED.** Prediction 1
        held, 3 held, and **2 is refuted in the direction that matters**: the
        cap does not increase the number of usable cells, it decreases them.
        Predictions were registered in note 018 before the mechanism existed.
        The full grid is not being run -- see the last section for why.


WHY
---
[g8-02](g8-02-when-the-statistics-are-real.txt) could only use two of its five
cells. At `zipf_s` 1.0 and above the ungated arm falls below the trivial floor,
so seven of fifteen cells divide by the gap between a working ceiling and a
broken floor. **Word frequencies sit near exponent 1.0 — the first cell it cannot
use.**

[Note 018](../../docs/notes/018-the-fast-store-has-no-brakes.md) says why. The
fast store is a geometric series in `decay`; repetition drives it toward
`1 / (1 - decay)`, about 277x a single binding here. Retrieval is linear in that
and the delta-rule update is quadratic, so it diverges. Measured without
training, as skew rises: memory norm 114 → 967, largest retrieval 137 → 3452.
(Those are the RUNAWAY PROBE's scales, not the model's -- see below. Measured
through the model the store is 2-5 at uniform and 10-50 at zipf 2.0.)

**So g8-02 could not test realistic skew because the model cannot survive
realistic skew.** Re-running it before fixing that reproduces the same broken
cells, which is why this comes first.


THE GRID
--------
  memory_cap   0 (off), and three values that bind
  zipf_s       0.0, 0.5, 1.0, 1.5, 2.0     the same axis g8-02 could not use
  arms         none, oracle, on-use, salience   unchanged from g8-02
  lr           0.02, 0.05, 0.1
  seeds        1, 2, 3

The cap values are chosen from the measured runaway, not guessed: the memory norm
reaches 114 at uniform filler, so a cap far above that cannot bind and a cap near
it binds only under repetition. **A pre-dispatch control must confirm each
candidate binds where intended and nowhere else**, because a cap that fires at
uniform statistics is mis-set rather than safe.


PREDICTIONS -- registered in note 018 before the mechanism existed
-------------------------------------------------------------------
1. **It stops the NaN.** Weak, close to arithmetic, and already shown at unit
   scale: with warnings as errors, a repeated-token sequence raises without the
   cap and completes with it.

2. **It stops or delays the collapse onto one token.** The collapse and the
   divergence may be the same event or two, and this separates them. **The
   measurement is how many cells become usable** — how far up the skew axis the
   ungated arm stays above the trivial floor. If it reaches `zipf_s` 1.0, the
   base-rate hypothesis becomes testable at realistic skew for the first time.

3. **It changes little at uniform statistics**, where the memory norm is 114 and
   a cap well above that never binds. If uniform results move, the cap is binding
   where nothing was wrong and the value is wrong.

4. **It does NOT rescue the gating result.** Recovery for `on-use` and `salience`
   should stay near zero wherever cells were already usable. **This is the
   loudest prediction and the reason the sweep is worth running carefully:**
   stability is not selectivity, and a fix that quietly improves the headline
   number is the most dangerous kind. If recovery rises in cells that were
   already valid, something is confounded and note 018 is wrong about what the
   cap does.

Most likely wrong: (2). The collapse may be a property of the delta rule under a
dominant class rather than of the store's magnitude, in which case bounding the
store changes the divergence and not the degeneracy.


WHAT WOULD REFUTE WHAT
-----------------------
- more cells usable, recovery flat -> the cap does its job and only its job. g8-02
                                      can be re-run and the base-rate hypothesis
                                      tested at realistic skew
- no more cells usable             -> collapse is not caused by magnitude.
                                      Prediction 2 wrong, and the corpus
                                      benchmark needs a different fix
- recovery RISES in usable cells   -> the cap is doing something other than
                                      bounding. Suspect it before celebrating
- uniform results move             -> mis-set cap; re-choose from the control


WHAT THIS CANNOT SETTLE
------------------------
**Whether the corpus benchmark will work.** A power law over a 28-token filler
alphabet shares one property with language. Surviving skew here is necessary and
nowhere near sufficient.

**Anything about gating.** Six mechanisms have failed and g8-03 established they
all acted on the wrong store. This sweep is about stability, and if it appears to
say anything about gating, see prediction 4.


RESULT -- from the pre-dispatch control, one seed, lr 0.02
============================================================
Ungated accuracy, which is what decides whether a cell is usable at all.
Trivial floor 0.34375.

    zipf_s   uncapped    cap 5    cap 10
       0.0      0.782    0.782     0.782
       0.5      0.763    0.763     0.763
       1.0      0.713    0.715     0.713
       1.5      0.351    0.290     0.328
       2.0      0.000    0.197     0.259

THE PREDICTIONS
----------------
1. **It stops the NaN -- HELD.** Uncapped at `zipf_s` 2.0 the readout diverges
   and the arm scores 0.000; capped it completes and scores 0.197 and 0.259.
   The divergence is real, reproducible, and the cap prevents it.

2. **It stops or delays the collapse -- REFUTED, in the direction that matters.**
   The measurement was **how many cells become usable**, and the answer is
   *fewer*. At `zipf_s` 1.5 the uncapped arm clears the floor at 0.351; capped it
   scores 0.290 and 0.328, both **below** it. A cell that was usable is now not.

   At 2.0 the cap lifts 0.000 to 0.259, which is a large rescue and still below
   the floor. Rescuing a broken cell into a differently broken cell does not add
   a usable one.

3. **It changes little at uniform statistics -- HELD, exactly.** 0.782 and 0.763
   to three decimals at `zipf_s` 0.0 and 0.5, capped or not. The cap is not
   binding where nothing is wrong, which is what a correctly-set bound should do.

4. **It does not rescue the gating result -- NOT TESTED.** The control measures
   only the ungated arm. It was the loudest prediction and it remains unanswered,
   which is a gap and not a pass.

WHY THE GRID IS NOT BEING RUN
------------------------------
The sweep existed to answer one question: does bounding the fast store make
realistic skew measurable? **The control answers it, and the answer is no.**

Running 45 more cells would produce recovery ratios in cells whose floor arm is
below the trivial floor -- which is exactly the error withdrawn from g8-01 and
g8-02 earlier today. The grid cannot contain a usable answer, so it is not worth
runner time. Recorded here rather than quietly dropped: a sweep that is not run
is a decision, and decisions belong in the file.

WHAT IT COSTS AND WHAT IT BUYS
--------------------------------
Costs: the fast-store cap is not the unblocker for realistic skew. **The corpus
benchmark stays blocked**, and it is now blocked by something better understood
rather than by something merely diagnosed.

Buys: two things worth having.

**The divergence and the collapse are different failures.** The cap removes the
divergence completely and the collapse barely at all, so a NaN and a
degeneracy-onto-one-token were never the same event. Note 018 said they might be
and that separating them was the point of prediction 2; they are separate.

**Bounding magnitude is not the mechanism.** The store growing large is a symptom
of repetition, not the cause of the failure. Something else about a dominant
class defeats the delta rule, and it survives the store being held small. That is
a sharper statement of the blocker than note 018 could make, and it rules out the
cheapest candidate fix.

The cap stays in, default off, because preventing a NaN is worth having on its
own -- and because g8-02's bottom rows can now be re-run without the divergence
confounding whatever else is happening there.
