Can a word and a picture reach the same concept?
================================================

Script: experiments/g36_01_a_picture_and_a_word.py
Status: PREDICTIONS COMMITTED, NOT YET RUN.


IN PLAIN TERMS
--------------
This is the test the whole grounding idea was built for, and it is the first time
the project has pointed it at something that is not made of symbols it wrote
itself.

Until now, a "picture" and a "word" were both just numbers, and two numbers
either match or they do not. That skips the hard part. A real concept is one word
and **hundreds of different pictures**, no two identical — a child does not see
the same dog twice.

So: handwritten digits. One concept is the digit three: the word *three*, and
several hundred pictures of threes, all different. Nobody tells the machine which
pictures go together, and nobody tells it which word goes with which pictures. It
sees a picture and hears a word at the same moment, over and over, with other
words being said that are not about it.

**The question is whether it can get from the word to the pictures.**

There are two separate ways this can fail and the project's own goals document
insists they must not be lumped together, so they are reported apart:

  - **Can it tell that two pictures of threes are the same kind of thing?**
    That is about seeing, and we are using a deliberately crude method for it.
  - **Given that, can it link the word to them?** That is the actual grounding
    question and it is the one this project is trying to answer.

If the second fails while the first works, the idea is in trouble. If the first
fails, this run says nothing about the second and we need a better way of seeing
before asking again.


WHAT IS BEING MEASURED
----------------------
    quantiser purity   share of pictures sitting in a code whose MAJORITY digit
                       is their own. The seeing half
    link purity        of the image codes a word's recovered class contains,
                       the share whose majority digit is that word's. The
                       grounding half
    reach              share of the ten words that reach ANY image code
    classes            mean recovered class size, to catch a collapse

**Chance for both purities is the largest class share, about 0.11**, and is
printed by the run rather than assumed. A purity near that is a mechanism that
has learned nothing.

`link purity` is recall-shaped in the same way every metric in this line has
been: a total collapse would put every code in every class and score well. `mean
class size` is the companion, and a collapse shows up there as a number near the
surface count.


MEASURED ON
-----------
Local, single process, Python 3.14, numpy for the quantiser only.

    images       4,000    MNIST training prefix, fetched by tools/fetch_mnist.py
    pixels         784    28x28 greyscale, raw, unnormalised beyond unit length
    words           10    one token per digit
    noise            2    OTHER WORDS per occasion -- things said in the room
                          that are not about the picture. NOT other pictures: a
                          learner looking at one thing sees one thing
    distractors      1    carried from g32-01, present every occasion
    quantiser  spherical k-means over unit pixel rows, `grouping.cluster`
    statistic  conditional, the C1-safe one
    bound      derived, `g33-04`'s winner
    seeds      0, 1, 2

Swept: `codes` in {20, 50, 100}, which is 200, 80 and 40 images per code.

**The quantiser is BORROWED and deliberately weak.** `DECISIONS.md` §1 records
John's ruling that a borrowed feature space is acceptable and possibly preferred,
and raw pixels are the crudest honest choice. The point is to measure the linking
against a quantiser whose quality is known and reported, not to build a good one.


PREDICTIONS
-----------
P1  THE SEEING HALF WORKS BADLY BUT NOT USELESSLY. Quantiser purity lands
    between 0.40 and 0.85 at every code count — far above chance 0.11 and far
    below 1.0, because raw pixels group by ink position rather than by shape.
    REFUTED IF near chance, in which case nothing downstream is readable and the
    run says nothing about grounding; or if near 1.0, which would be
    suspiciously good for raw pixels and would mean checking for a leak before
    believing anything.

P2  THE GATE. Link purity is far above chance — above **0.50** — so a word's
    recovered class holds image codes of its own digit rather than of any digit.
    REFUTED IF link purity is near chance 0.11. **That is G7's falsifier firing**:
    the same concept could not be reached from two modalities, and `GOALS.md`'s
    answer to symbol grounding would be failing on the first real test of it.

P3  LINKING CANNOT BEAT SEEING. Link purity is at or below quantiser purity,
    because a word cannot distinguish two image codes that the quantiser has
    already confused.
    REFUTED IF link purity EXCEEDS quantiser purity by a clear margin. **That
    refutation would be the most interesting outcome available here**: it would
    mean the word REPAIRS the quantiser — two codes that both co-occur with
    *three* get linked into one concept even though the pixels never said they
    belonged together — and that is the multimodal claim doing real work rather
    than riding on the front end.

P4  MORE CODES CUTS BOTH WAYS. Raising `codes` raises quantiser purity (finer
    clusters are purer) and lowers the occasions per code, so link purity peaks
    in the middle rather than at 100. `g32-02` measured about 16 occasions per
    concept as the threshold, and 100 codes leaves 40 images each.
    REFUTED IF link purity rises monotonically with codes, which would say the
    occasion threshold does not bind here and the quantiser is the only limit.
