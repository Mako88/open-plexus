THE ORDERING, AND IT IS THE RESULT
-----------------------------------
Each arm at its OWN best setting, chosen on held-out training text:

    floor                9.185     no grouping
    stratified-128       9.185     only the rare tail grouped
    current-128          9.252     one coordinate grouped
    context-128          9.591     the other coordinate grouped
    concept-128          9.985     both coordinates grouped

    word unigram         8.068     still 1.12 bits ahead of the best arm

**Every step of address collapse costs accuracy, and none of it buys anything.**
The arms are ordered exactly by how much resolution the grouping destroys, and
the ordering is monotone across five arms and four distinct address-space sizes:

    arm              addresses   recurrence   bits
    floor               36,299         2.48   9.185
    stratified-128      19,490         4.62   9.185
    current-128         12,776         7.04   9.252
    context-128         13,113         6.86   9.591
    concept-128          3,438        26.18   9.985

Recurrence rises tenfold across that table. It buys nothing anywhere.

**So P1's gate fails at K=128 in five different ways**, and it fails in the shape
that refutes the account rather than the wiring: the address space collapsed
exactly as designed, the rail passes at every arm, and the model got worse.

`stratified` being IDENTICAL to the floor to three decimals at four rates is the
one result here that is not about the mechanism, and it raised the question in
the next section.


THE QUESTION PASS 2 RAISED, AND A PREDICTION REGISTERED BEFORE LOOKING
-----------------------------------------------------------------------
`floor` and `stratified-128` agree to three decimals at four different rates.
**Two different addressing schemes cannot agree that precisely unless what
distinguishes them has stopped mattering** -- so the small-rate regime may be one
where the store contributes almost nothing and the readout's bias is doing the
work.

If so, "the model learns word-level text better than we thought" is a statement
about a unigram-shaped prior rather than about the memory, and every arm
comparison at that rate is between two inert models.

The ablation is `nostore`: nothing is ever written, so every retrieval is zero,
`Wo` receives a delta proportional to zero and never moves, and the logits are
the learned bias alone.

  **PREDICTED, BEFORE THE RUN RETURNED:** `nostore` lands near the unigram at
  8.068 -- and therefore BETTER than the tuned floor's 9.217. That would mean
  the store is a NET COST at word level, and that lowering the learning rate
  helps by reducing how much damage it does.

  What would refute it: `nostore` landing at or above 10.5, near uniform. That
  says the bias alone learns little, the store carries the 1.5 bits between
  there and 9.217, and the tuned floor is a real result about the memory.

MEASURED: **9.1865**, at lr 5e-6 and cap 5, against the tuned floor's **9.185**.

SCORED: **the prediction is REFUTED in its number and CONFIRMED in its point**,
        and the difference between those matters.

        Predicted 8.068 -- that the bias learns a unigram and the store is a net
        cost. Measured 9.187: the bias does NOT learn a unigram, it lands 1.12
        bits worse than counting. So the "store is actively harmful" half is
        wrong at this rate.

        What is right, and it is the larger half: **the store contributes
        NOTHING.** The tuned floor and its own no-store ablation agree to three
        decimals. Every word-level bit this model earns is the readout bias.

        The reason `floor` and `stratified-128` agreed so exactly is now plain:
        they were both reporting the same bias.


WHAT THAT DOES TO THE REST OF THIS RECORD
------------------------------------------
**The floor was never a floor.** It is a learned prior with an inert memory
attached, and the 0.98 bits the learning rate "recovered" is the model
progressively switching the store OFF: at lr 0.05 the store drags the model to
10.108, at 5e-6 its contribution reaches zero, and it is never positive.

    lr 0.05     floor 10.108   store is HARMFUL, by 0.92 against nostore
    lr 5e-6     floor  9.185   store is INERT, to three decimals
    (no rate measured, from 5e-4 down to 2e-6, where it is positive)

So the arm ordering above reads differently and more simply. Grouping does not
"cost resolution the store needed" -- **the store was not using any resolution.**
The groupings that scored worse than the floor did so by making an inert
component harmful again, which is why the ordering tracks how much collapse each
one applies.

**And it puts g17-01's whole account in question, not just its number.** The
account was that word-level text fails because the address space is too sparse
for the store to superpose anything reusable. That may be true and it is no
longer the binding constraint: at the tuned rate the store is not superposing
anything at all, reusable or not.

The next question is therefore not about addressing. It is: **is there any rate,
width or key scheme at which the store contributes a single positive bit at word
level?** `nostore` is now an arm of g18-01 so that no later table can be read
without it.
